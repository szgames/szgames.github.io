About Moon Crashers

The release date for Moon Crashers was July 27, 2026, and the game is now available on PC. This four-player cooperative adventure tasks a stranded squad with scavenging ship parts and raw minerals across a hazardous lunar surface to build a functional escape rocket.

Success depends on managing low-gravity physics while operating janky moon buggies and jetpacks to transport heavy salvage. The primary challenge lies in the physics-based construction; a poorly welded ship will fail during the return journey, forcing the team to balance speed with careful engineering. It serves as a mechanical test of coordination where environmental hazards and proximity voice chat define the chaos of the repair process.

Pick this up if you want a physics-heavy co-op builder where one mistake can scrap the entire mission.
