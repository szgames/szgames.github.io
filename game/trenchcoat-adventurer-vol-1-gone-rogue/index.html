About Trenchcoat Adventurer Vol 1 : Gone Rogue

Trenchcoat Adventurer Vol 1 : Gone Rogue is now available on PC following its July 21, 2026, release date. This turn-based dungeon crawler iterates on the blobber formula by having you control three kobolds stacked on top of each other inside a single coat. You will navigate thirty hand-crafted floors where the difficulty spikes sharply as the enemy AI adapts to your tactics.

Survival hinges on brutal inventory management where your kobolds must literally eat items to clear space or gain buffs. Between slippery ice tiles and chaos altars that can curse your gear, positioning and risk assessment are more important than raw stats. The game uses permadeath to ensure every slide into a fire trap or failed gamble at a forge carries permanent consequences for your trio. It is a focused, punishing RPG for those who want a tight grid-based challenge with a weird mechanical hook. Grab this if you enjoy high-stakes turn-based crawling and tight inventory constraints.
