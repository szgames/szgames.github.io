About Gears of War: E-Day

The release date for Gears of War: E-Day is October 6, 2026, for PC and Xbox platforms. This prequel shifts the series back fourteen years before the original game, focusing on the initial Locust invasion through the eyes of a younger Marcus Fenix and Dominic Santiago.

The Coalition is moving the franchise toward its horror roots with this origin story, built in Unreal Engine 5 to emphasize the grim reality of the subterranean threat. While the campaign focuses on the personal toll of Emergence Day, the package includes Horde Siege, a class-based PvE mode expanded for larger maps and world boss encounters. By moving away from the later planetary conflicts to a localized, desperate urban defense, the game attempts to recapture the heavy, claustrophobic tension that defined the early series. It is a mandatory pickup for long-term fans wanting to see the bond between Marcus and Dom forge under fire.
