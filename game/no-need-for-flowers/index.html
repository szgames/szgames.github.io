About No Need for Flowers

The release date for No Need for Flowers was August 7, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. This arcade-style scrolling shooter tasks you with dodging colorful bullet patterns as powerful dragon priestesses, all while uncovering a tragic story of acceptance through expressive characters and dialogue.

With three playable characters, intense boss battles testing skill and memory, and seven unique stages, expect demanding challenges. The inclusion of a practice mode, replays, and an unlockable bestiary ensures a deep experience for players ready to master its bullet hell mechanics. Skill-focused players seeking a challenging bullet hell with a meaningful story should definitely check out this unique title.
