About OFFICE KEEPER

The release date for OFFICE KEEPER is July 27, 2026, and it is launching on PC. This indie simulation blends casual score-attack mechanics with a horror-lite premise, challenging you to tidy up an abandoned building while avoiding a lethal presence stalking the corridors.

Success depends on efficient resource management during five-to-ten-minute shifts. You must scavenge for flashlight batteries and speed-boosting sprays while toggling between a vacuum and a cloth to clear randomized filth patterns. The realistic cleaning system tracks your progress exactly where you wipe, making the pressure to hit the target score before the entity catches you a genuine test of nerve. Every run features different dust placements to keep the leaderboard competition unpredictable.

Fans of bite-sized horror games should wishlist this for a tense but quick arcade experience.
