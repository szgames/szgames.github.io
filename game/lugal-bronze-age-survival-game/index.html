About Lugal: Bronze Age Survival Game

The release date for Lugal: Bronze Age Survival Game is August 17, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This ambitious survival game challenges players to lead a prehistoric tribe from hunter-gatherers through the Stone Age into the Bronze Age, ultimately founding a city, uniting states, and constructing a Great Ziggurat to become a recognized Lugal, or king.

Beyond standard resource gathering and crafting, Lugal integrates city-building and a unique quest for political legitimacy, exploring why ancient civilizations undertook massive construction projects. It blends co-op survival mechanics with an extensive questline, offering a grand progression from a small village to a powerful state through technology discovery and conflict. Consider this a definite wishlist for fans of deep historical simulation blended with engaging survival craft elements.
