About Boreal Tenebrae Act 0: Sundowning

The release date for Boreal Tenebrae Act 0: Sundowning on PC was July 30, 2026.

This lo-fi, analog-horror adventure uses PSX-style visuals to depict the surreal collapse of Tamarack Narrows. Play through interwoven perspectives as an unknown visitor arrives during a centennial celebration, turning salvation into something far worse. Fans of unsettling, fixed-angle narrative horror with a strong retro aesthetic should check out this intriguing prequel.
