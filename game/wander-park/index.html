About Wander Park

The release date for Wander Park on PC is July 2026. This casual indie title offers an endless supply of procedurally generated mazes set within an abstract park, designed specifically for relaxation rather than traditional challenge. You simply wander to find the exit fountain, with no story or level progression to distract from the tranquil experience.

Players can customize the park's ambiance, adjusting time of day and weather, making it an ideal backdrop for meditative walks or background audio. While it largely eschews traditional goals, an optional daily park speedrun caters to competitive maze solvers. If you need a digital escape from complex gameplay, Wander Park is worth watching.
