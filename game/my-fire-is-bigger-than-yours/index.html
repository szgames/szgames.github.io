About My Fire Is Bigger Than Yours

The release date for My Fire Is Bigger Than Yours is August 30, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

This physics-based incremental strategy game dares players to grow a tiny campfire into a celestial force, ultimately eclipsing the Sun. Success means discovering chaotic chain reactions and experimenting with over 200 upgrades to craft unique builds, even starting a cult to feed your ever-growing flame as you expand to the Moon. Expect a focused, escalating experience across three distinct phases. Fans of innovative idle games with deep strategic choices should definitely wishlist this.
