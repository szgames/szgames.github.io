About Mad Merchant

The release date for Mad Merchant is July 20, 2026, on PC. This simulation-strategy hybrid puts you in command of a modular merchant train navigating a hostile dystopian wasteland where your survival depends on balancing mechanical upkeep with heavy firepower.

You must manage real-time wagon status and system integrity while fending off rogue drone swarms using either automated turrets or manual targeting. The core loop involves a high-stakes trade-off between speed and safety, forcing you to choose between dangerous shortcuts or fuel-intensive long routes to reach the next trade haven. Success relies on your ability to haggle for parts and precisely upgrade your rolling fortress before the next cross-border push. It is a focused incremental management experience for those who want to optimize a mobile base under constant pressure.

Buy this if you want a gritty, rail-bound management challenge with tactical combat.
