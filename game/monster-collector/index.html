About Monster Collector

The release date for Monster Collector is August 27, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This collection-focused RPG sets itself apart by prioritizing the satisfaction of discovery and catalog completion over conventional power progression.

Players will explore 20 distinct regions in search of 1,020 unique monsters, including rare variants and ultra-rare creatures, all to fill their Monster Catalog. Planning your next hunt involves choosing regions and equipment to efficiently track down missing entries and complete your collection. If the thrill of discovery and catalog completion is your primary draw, Monster Collector is a clear wishlist for dedicated monster collectors.
