About No chicken wants to die

The release date for No chicken wants to die is July 23, 2026, on PC. This indie title puts you in control of a chick wielding a manure fork to defend a coop against waves of zombies.

While the premise looks light, the progression system is surprisingly dense. The game features a massive upgrade tree with over 100 nodes, allowing for specialized builds as you work through its 10 stages. It is designed as a short, focused experience that aims for a full completion and all achievements in roughly two hours. The variety comes from the chick family's different abilities, which shift the math of the incremental combat as the enemy density scales up.

Pick this up for a quick, satisfying loop of power scaling and base defense.
