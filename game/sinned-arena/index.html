About Sinned Arena

The release date for Sinned Arena is July 28, 2026, on PC. This title is a classic arena shooter that traps you in a hellish landscape to fight other souls for the entertainment of demons. It focuses on the high-speed, twitch-reflex movement that defined the genre in the late 90s, demanding constant movement to survive the gauntlet.

The package includes a single-player campaign that escalates in difficulty and a skirmish mode featuring mutators and five distinct match types, including Heartcore and Instagib. While it lacks a multiplayer component, the inclusion of objective-based modes and customizable bot matches provides a specific training ground for those who miss the era of Quake and Unreal Tournament. It is a solid choice for solo players who want a pure mechanical challenge without online queues. Buy this if you want a fast, focused throwback to old-school deathmatch design.
