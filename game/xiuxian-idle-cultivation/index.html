About XiuXian Idle Cultivation

The release date for XiuXian Idle Cultivation is July 23, 2026, on PC. This simulation RPG is designed specifically as a transparent desktop overlay, allowing you to manage your progression while handling other tasks or working in different windows.

Instead of demanding your full attention, the game operates through a compact combat bar where your character automatically grinds for materials and equipment across various zones like the Frosty Mist Marsh and Starfall Desert. The depth lies in how you layer skill paths, such as physical or sword cultivation, to push through the traditional progression from Qi Refining to the rank of True Immortal. It is a specialized tool for those who want the satisfaction of an RPG loot cycle and steady character growth without the distraction of a full-screen interface.

Keep this running in the background if you want a low-maintenance progression loop to occupy your desktop.
