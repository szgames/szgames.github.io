About Log of Adventurers

The release date for Log of Adventurers on PC is August 2026. This free-to-play text-driven online RPG transports players to a post-flood world on the continent of Alvasa, where the old world has been abandoned by its creator. Players choose their survival path, engaging in a wide array of activities from gathering resources and crafting equipment to taming creatures, studying magic, or exploring vast, varied regions.

Its skill-based progression and extensive exploration across plains, deserts, ice seas, and ancient ruins are meticulously recorded in your personal log, detailing discoveries, battles, and lore. The game offers a deep, intricate experience for those who prefer player choice and world interaction over real-time action. Consider a download if you seek a persistent, text-heavy online survival adventure.
