About Backrooms Manager - Together

The release date for Backrooms Manager - Together was August 14, 2026, on PC. In this six-player cooperative scavenge-and-survive horror game, you are a courier trapped in a liminal dimension under a brutal Z.O.L. corporate contract. You have exactly 49 days to clear your team debt by processing scrap and delivering cargo, but there is a permanent penalty for failure: when a crew member dies, they do not respawn, while their accumulated expenses remain on the group balance.

Success depends on maintaining a positive account balance during audits that occur every three days. The pressure comes from managing the team budget against the high cost of equipment and the threat of permanent character loss in shifting, procedurally generated corridors. This is a high-stakes management hybrid that prioritizes fiscal survival as much as physical safety. Groups who enjoy heavy coordination and the risk of permanent failure should play this now.

Gather a reliable crew and manage your way out of a corporate nightmare.
