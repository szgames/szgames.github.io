About The Old Flesh of Nuraga

The release date for The Old Flesh of Nuraga was August 27, 2026, and the game is now available exclusively on PC. This first-person psychological horror adventure strands you in a desert of ancient ruins, tasked with a treasure hunt alongside your increasingly deranged grandfather.

As he fixates on ominous relics and his own wishes, a disembodied voice whispers to you, adding another layer of dread to this brief 1-2 hour experience. Prepare to face unsettling truths and decide if some things should stay buried. If a compact, narrative-driven horror focusing on psychological tension and unsettling family dynamics appeals, make time for this.
