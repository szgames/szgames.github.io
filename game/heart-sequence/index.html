About Heart sequence

The release date for Heart sequence is August 3, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This roguelike deckbuilder introduces a unique twist where your chosen skills are executed in time with an internal heartbeat rhythm, demanding careful sequencing and strategic foresight to create powerful synergies.

Success in the game hinges on adapting your deck throughout dungeon runs, making key decisions in events, and discovering new items to devise fresh strategies against multiple unique bosses. Its novel heartbeat mechanic elevates standard deckbuilding into a true test of timing and tactical planning. Fans of innovative strategy roguelikes should definitely wishlist this for PC.
