About HELP FROG

The release date for HELP FROG on PC is July 21, 2026. This physics-based action title centers on a high-stakes ascent where you control a frog trapped inside a snail shell. Progression relies entirely on using your tongue to latch onto the environment and swing upward toward a distant summit.

The appeal lies in its punishing simplicity. There are no combat encounters or power-ups to bail you out; instead, you must master momentum and friction to avoid a total reset. One mistimed swing or failed latch sends you plummeting back to the start, placing it firmly in the niche of high-frustration climbing games. It is a pure test of patience and precise movement mechanics. Fans of high-difficulty platformers should wishlist this for a July launch.
