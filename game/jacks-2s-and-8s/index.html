About Jack's, 2's & 8's

The release date for Jack's, 2's & 8's is July 27, 2026, on PC. This indie title digitizes the classic shedding card game, focusing on the specific house rules that make kitchen-table matches notoriously competitive.

You must match rank or suit to empty your hand, using specialty cards to disrupt your opponents. Twos stack to force massive card draws on the next player, sevens skip turns, and eights allow for consecutive plays, while the versatile Jack can change the active suit at any time. The game supports local pass-the-mouse play, private online lobbies for four players, or solo matches against AI personalities with distinct playstyles. With its hand-drawn deck and lamplit aesthetic, the focus is entirely on the tension of the snowballing draw pile.

A solid choice for groups looking for a familiar, low-stress digital card night or a quick competitive fix against the computer.
