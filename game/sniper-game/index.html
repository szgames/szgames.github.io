About Sniper Game

The release date for Sniper Game on PC was July 22, 2026. This first-person marksman simulator focuses entirely on a singular, competitive goal: securing the highest score on a global leaderboard by eliminating escalating waves of targets from two distinct vantage points.

Instead of a traditional narrative campaign, Coral Art Studio has opted for a pure skill-based format where every player faces the exact same conditions to ensure fair competition. You choose between the long-range safety of the Sniper Building or the increased pressure of the Enemy Building, with each position maintaining its own ranking system. The risk here lies in the limited scope; it is a specialized experience that lives or dies on the quality of its mechanical precision and the activity of its community.

Serious competitive shooters can now climb up the world standings.
