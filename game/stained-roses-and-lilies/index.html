About Stained Roses & Lilies

The release date for Stained Roses and Lilies is July 23, 2026, on PC. This indie RPG follows the slow dissolution of a family unit after a fitness coach enters the lives of the protagonist's mother and sister. What begins as a routine domestic setting shifts into a darker narrative of corruption and social manipulation, as the protagonist watches the people closest to him change under an external influence.

The story focuses on Saeko, a divorced mother, and Nadeko, an academically gifted sister, as their personalities are systematically reshaped. Unlike traditional RPGs focused on combat, this title emphasizes the psychological tension of losing one's support system and the irreversible transformation of a quiet home. The challenge lies in navigating these shifting relationships and determining if the descent into darkness can be halted before the family dynamic is destroyed forever. This is a targeted choice for players who prefer slow-burn narrative dramas centered on domestic themes and character corruption.

Wishlist this for a focused, character-driven psychological RPG.
