About Stick Legion

The release date for Stick Legion is August 18, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This strategy simulation cleverly redefines income as a literal battle line, where every recruited warrior fights and dies gloriously to push your legion further across eight distinct realms. Its innovative Mini Mode lets you dock the ongoing war in a screen corner, allowing your army to battle on and earn resources while you focus on other tasks.

While your legions can conquer realms hands-off, the Command system allows active players to tap the battlefield to bank charge and unleash powerful Volley Orders, accelerating your advance. You’ll upgrade 9 unique warbands and invest hard-won Glory into Elders for permanent boosts. Vanishline Games commits to a respectful design, offering a complete game with no ads or microtransactions and an actual ending. Players seeking a unique blend of idle progression and active strategic influence will find Stick Legion a definite purchase.
