About SkyFab

The release date for SkyFab is August 17, 2026, launching on PC. This first-person voxel survival game strands you on a floating island, tasking you with transforming a desolate rock into an industrial space station through advanced engineering.

Success hinges on designing complex production lines, managing dynamic power grids, and automating everything from sifting dirt to high-tech machinery. The unique Anvil System promises a tactile crafting experience as you hammer ingots. If you enjoy deep automation and survival, this is a definite wishlist.
