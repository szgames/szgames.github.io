About Italia 1915–1918 : Great War Logistics

The release date for Italia 1915–1918 : Great War Logistics is July 23, 2026, on PC. While most World War I titles focus on the mud and blood of the trenches, this turn-based management game shifts the perspective to the encampments of the Italian Army. You are responsible for the fragile infrastructure that sustains the front, managing buildings like the Command Office, Field Hospital, and Supply Depot as the enemy lines gradually approach.

Success depends on balancing your resources through day and night cycles to keep your unit functional and morale high. The focus on logistics over direct combat creates a methodical pace where every building upgrade and courier run directly impacts the survival of your men. It is a specific look at the friction of war behind the front line.

Historical strategy fans should wishlist this for its unique focus on the Italian front and camp management.
