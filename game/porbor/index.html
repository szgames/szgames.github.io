About PorBor

The release date for PorBor was July 24, 2026, for PC. This indie metroidvania leans heavily into a high-risk combat system where drawing blood from enemies generates a resource called Kilt. Because this substance is physically pushed away from you upon creation, you are forced to stay in the pocket and maintain aggressive, close-quarters pressure to stay healthy and strong.

As Bor, you navigate a non-linear world where nature has overgrown a broken civilization. The challenge lies in the precise platforming and the reactionary boss fights that demand quick reflexes. Between the dozen distinct zones and the sanity-frayed characters you meet, the game focuses on a snappy, untethered movement style that makes the backtracking and exploration feel fluid. This is a clear choice for players who prefer aggressive combat mechanics over passive exploration. If you want a fast-paced take on the genre, this game is available now.
