About Gravitaws

The release date for Gravitaws was September 1, 2026 on PC. This retro-style stage shooter channels NES-era Jaws, pitting players as ex-secret agent and science teacher John Sheider against an alien invader after a meteor strike. Dive deep beneath the waves, explore a dynamic overworld, and uncover research materials while evading the mysterious marine threat.

Fight across air, reel, and sea, supported by Captain Shaw and Miss Dreyfuss. The game also provides accessibility settings like auto-fire and auto-movement for mouse-only gameplay, full keybinding, and customizable cursor colors. Fans of classic action-adventure with a unique premise should certainly play this one.
