About VORTEX-G: Anti-Gravity Racing & Track Editor

The release date for VORTEX-G: Anti-Gravity Racing & Track Editor was July 25, 2026, for PC. This high-velocity racer targets the specific physics-based handling of the 90s and 2000s anti-gravity era, and launched with a massive 117-track roster and nine arcade-style tournaments to conquer. Victory unlocks a trophy hall where you can inspect your awards in a specialized 360-degree workshop view.

What distinguishes this from a standard retro-tribute is the integration of a full-scale track editor and a specialized Taxi Mode. Between races, you can shift from professional competition to urban exploration, navigating a futuristic city on foot or by ship to collect hidden orbs and unlock an anime art gallery. It is a rare combination of precision racing and open-ended creative tools. Fans of classic AG racers should add this to their collection for the depth of its content alone.
