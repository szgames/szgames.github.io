About Innocent Depths:The Dungeon Sweeper

The release date for Innocent Depths:The Dungeon Sweeper was July 22, 2026, on PC. This single-player roguelike combines dungeon crawling with logic puzzles, tasking you with clearing a path through block-filled layers using Minesweeper-style number clues and radar scans to identify hidden threats.

While the focus is on navigation, the game shifts into a tactical RPG when monsters block your progress. You will need to manage a loadout of relics and weapons to survive these unavoidable encounters, making the equipment choices you find in treasure chests just as vital as your ability to read the grid. It is a specific blend of deduction and character building that rewards players who prefer a methodical, turn-based pace over reflex-heavy action.

Fans of logic puzzles and traditional dungeon delving should check out this launch.
