About Mall: Tidy Up Together

The release date for Mall: Tidy Up Together was August 21, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This first-person organization simulation challenges you to bring order to a sprawling mall, shelving over 3,600 products across diverse stores from supermarkets to video game shops.

Unlock unique skills like auto-placement and product highlighting to streamline the tidying process, or test your mastery in "No-Skill Mode" and "Sandbox Mode." Play solo, team up for online co-op, or go head-to-head in PvP for ultimate retail tidiness bragging rights. If finding satisfaction in meticulous order and competitive clean-up appeals, this one is available for purchase.
