About SlimeKitten

The release date for SlimeKitten is August 28, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This 2D precision platformer stands out with a distinct movement system where you aim each jump with a trajectory line, dialing in power and angle to stick to walls and flow through dozens of handcrafted levels.

Players can approach its stages either as physics puzzles, planning every leap meticulously, or chase flawless speedruns thanks to instant respawns and built-in timers. It's cute on the outside but offers a merciless challenge for those who crave tight, responsive controls. If you enjoy demanding platformers that reward mastery, mark SlimeKitten for purchase on release.
