About Just Skill Shooter: Japan

The release date for Just Skill Shooter: Japan is August 2026, arriving exclusively on PC. This first-person shooter plunges players into a high-stakes combat scenario across the striking, contrasting landscapes of modern urban alleys and ancient Japanese shrines. Success hinges on raw skill, demanding precision and strategic use of a varied arsenal against cunning enemy AI.

The game leans into its title, requiring mastery of rifles, shotguns, and pistols to adapt to both tight corridors and open courtyards as a rogue faction turns peaceful streets into a warzone.
