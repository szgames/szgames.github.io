About HEXSTORM: Tears of Arcadia

The release date for HEXSTORM: Tears of Arcadia was August 3, 2026, and it is now available on PC. This strategy title shifts the tower defense formula away from static paths and toward managing a 360-degree defensive perimeter around your flagship, the Arcadia.

Survival hinges on an interconnected power grid rather than isolated turrets. You must expand a network of collectors to fuel your economy and power your weapons, but expanding too fast creates vulnerabilities that enemies can exploit from any angle. The roguelite structure ensures that resource management and placement errors carry permanent consequences throughout a run, making the link between your power modules and turrets your most critical asset. Strategy fans who prefer spatial management and infrastructure planning over simple chokepoint building can now experience this. A high-stakes airborne siege awaits.
