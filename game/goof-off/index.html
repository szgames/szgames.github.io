About Goof Off

The release date for Goof Off was July 24, 2026, and the game is available exclusively for PC. This indie party fighter pits ameba-like creatures against one another in an underground warehouse, emphasizing fast-paced local multiplayer brawling.

Success relies on looting crates to find a pool of over 50 unpredictable weapons, ranging from literal fish to physics-bending black holes. Players can customize their approach using costume-based power-ups that grant specific traits like high-jump mobility or life-leeching attacks. Map hazards like lava and spikes force constant movement, turning every match into a chaotic scramble to secure the best gear and stay on the platforms. Grab a friend for couch-play madness now that this has launched in the summer of 2026.
