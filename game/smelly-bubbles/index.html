About Smelly Bubbles

The release date for Smelly Bubbles was August 7, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. This casual game throws players into a pool with friends, tasking them with identifying which player was responsible for a 'fart' through rounds of voting and elimination.

Beyond the core social deduction, players also gather leaves to clean the pool and can interact with toys, adding light, cooperative objectives to the voice-chat driven chaos. If you enjoy lighthearted social deduction with friends and absurd premises, it is available now.
