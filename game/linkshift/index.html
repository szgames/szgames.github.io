About Linkshift

The release date for Linkshift was July 22, 2026, on PC. This roguelite puzzle hybrid focuses on chaining nodes and stacking randomized upgrades to trigger high-scoring screen clears. While the handcrafted pixel art leans into a psychedelic aesthetic, the strategy is grounded in finding synergies between passive relics and active boosters to break the standard scoring rules.

Success depends on how well you can tinker with your build to sustain a flow state across different game modes, ranging from a relaxed casual pace to a more demanding exploration mode. Because it features full controller and Steam Deck support at launch, it is a strong pick for players who want a portable, high-frame-rate arcade experience with deep leaderboard competition. Buy this if you want a fast-paced strategy game that rewards experimentation and combo-heavy puzzle solving.
