About Manggou

The release date for Manggou is July 24, 2026, arriving on PC. This high-speed 2D side-scroller puts you in a relentless chase through a primal-tech jungle, where a massive durian pursues you with increasing speed. Your survival depends on maintaining momentum through parkour and precision mid-air slashes as you navigate a landscape filled with fruit-based monsters and hazardous traps.

Success relies on chaining gadgets and turning the environment against your pursuer rather than just running away. Between the frantic sprints, you will scavenge resources and upgrade your abilities to prepare for an eventual boss confrontation that ends the pursuit. It is a focused test of reflexes and combo management for those who enjoy tight, momentum-heavy action. Grab this if you want a punishing, rhythmic arcade challenge.
