About The Life and Suffering of Prince Jerian

The release date for The Life and Suffering of Prince Jerian is July 20, 2026, on PC. As the heir to the Blessed Arknian Empire, you are tasked with navigating a rigid caste system dictated by stern deities known as the Twins. While commoners suffer and nobles rule, your role as future emperor forces a choice between upholding a centuries-old social order or attempting to reform a world defined by its harsh punishments.

Schisma Games returns to the bleak universe established in Sir Brante, shifting the perspective from a struggling commoner to a powerful royal burdened by political expectations. The challenge lies in the attrition of your personal convictions against the needs of the state, where every decree carries a permanent cost for the citizenry. It is a dense, choice-driven experience for those who prefer weighing moral consequences over mechanical combat. Buy this on launch if you want a uncompromising look at the price of absolute power.
