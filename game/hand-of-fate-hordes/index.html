About Hand of Fate: Hordes

The release date for Hand of Fate: Hordes was July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by Spitfire Interactive, this title shifts the series' traditional combat toward a bullet-heaven auto-shooter while retaining its core deckbuilding mechanics.

Instead of the third-person brawling seen in previous entries, you manage four distinct hero classes—Trickster, Goblin, Warrior, and Rebel—to survive waves of enemies within the Shadow Vaults. The presence of Anthony Skordi reprising his role as the Dealer ensures the high-stakes atmospheric tension remains intact, even as the gameplay moves toward high-density horde survival and rogue-lite progression. The strategic challenge lies in choosing cards that modify the environment and your character's gear to survive until you can break through the 13th Gate.

Fans of the original series should prepare for a faster, more chaotic loop that prioritizes build synergy over individual strikes.
