About Reap and Rush

The release date for Reap and Rush was July 21, 2026, and it is available on PC. You take on the role of an underworld envoy sent to the mortal realm to fix a broken reincarnation system and locate a missing deity. This action roguelike focuses on managing high-density waves of Yaoguai while navigating a bureaucratic mess in the afterlife.

Combat relies on creating synergies between fate stones and yin spells, but your progress is also tied to non-combat interactions. You will spend underworld currency on paper craft upgrades and pause your soul-harvesting to solve the daily problems of fox spirits and misplaced gods. It is a mix of visceral crowd clearing and light RPG decision-making. Check this out if you are looking for a roguelike that leans heavily into Chinese mythology and specific gear synergies.
