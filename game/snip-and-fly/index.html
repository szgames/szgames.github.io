About Snip & Fly

The release date for Snip and Fly is July 23, 2026, on PC. This indie title uses a physics-based rope-cutting mechanic where you must swipe to free trapped birds while ensuring predatory eagles remain tethered to the ground. A single mistake triggers a level-ending failure, as any freed eagle will immediately hunt the rest of your flock.

The game distinguishes itself with a hand-drawn aesthetic on manuscript-style paper and a 100-level campaign that rewards speed and precision with medal rankings. Beyond the main puzzles, you can test your efficiency in a daily challenge mode or push for survival in endless play. It is a focused, low-pressure experience built around quick thinking and precise mouse movements. Puzzle fans looking for a clean, mechanical challenge should wishlist this for a launch day purchase.
