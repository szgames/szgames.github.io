About Catastrophic

The release date for Catastrophic was July 30, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This 3D survival game unleashes feline chaos, tasking players with controlling adorable kittens against waves of enemies. Success hinges on crafting devastating synergies within a strictly limited inventory, allowing only three weapons and three passive items.

Despite the cute premise, Catastrophic promises depth with multiplicative stats that exponentially boost your arsenal, pushing you to become a 'feline god' as you face unique bosses across three distinct maps. For action-casual fans eager for strategic buildcraft and cute creature survival, Catastrophic is definitely one to play.
