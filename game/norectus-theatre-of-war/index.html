About Norectus: Theatre of War

The release date for Norectus: Theatre of War is July 24, 2026, on PC. This free-to-play strategy card game blends traditional deckbuilding with a dedicated secondary mode that operates entirely as an auto-battler.

You will command one of five distinct nations, managing crystal resources to deploy units or holding back tactics to counter enemy movements. While the ranked 1v1 duels provide the competitive foundation, the inclusion of a 100-floor roguelike tower and an eight-player auto-chess mode ensures there is a significant amount of variety for a single client. The real challenge lies in the formation-based lane positioning and the timing of your keyword combos. It is a dense package for anyone who wants both direct tactical control and passive drafting strategy in one place. Buy this if you want a card game that successfully balances high-stakes dueling with casual, automated play.
