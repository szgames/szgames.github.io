About Minky

The release date for Minky was August 7, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This indie adventure plunges you into a bizarre, glitch-ridden video game designed by a lonely psycho named Minky, who has somehow kidnapped your consciousness.

Your only path to reality is to exploit his poorly programmed challenges and navigate a world where terrifying horror ambience suddenly gives way to hyper-upbeat music, complete with Minky's mom yelling in the background. For players seeking a truly unique, meta-narrative driven experience with chaotic, unpredictable design, Minky is one to play.
