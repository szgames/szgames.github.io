About Remothered: Red Nun's Legacy

The release date for Remothered: Red Nun's Legacy is October 26, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This third-person survival horror title serves as the definitive conclusion to the Red Nun saga, merging the stealth and psychological tension of classic genre entries with innovative perception and hypnosis mechanics. Players will need to master retrocognition and moth manipulation to twist environments and reveal forgotten memories.

Investigate the secrets of the Cristo Morente convent, solving puzzles while evading horrific Stalkers, with every mistake potentially fatal. The game features an original soundtrack by legendary composer Akira Yamaoka, and voice performances from BAFTA winner Cissy Jones and The Game Awards winner Maggie Robertson, ensuring an intense and polished experience. Fans seeking a fresh take on psychological horror should mark their calendars for this one.
