About Necrowhisper

The release date for Necrowhisper was September 6, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This is a tense investigative horror experience, playable solo or in 4-player co-op, where you must uncover the truth behind a spirit's death before a lurking entity finds you first.

Players will search detailed, atmospheric environments for procedurally placed clues, notes, and evidence to piece together the killer's identity, time, place, and method. The emphasis on real-time voice chat for co-op coordination adds a layer of dread, making isolation a constant risk as something learns and roams. Horror fans seeking a chilling deduction challenge with friends should definitely play this.
