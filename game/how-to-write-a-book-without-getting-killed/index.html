About How to Write a Book without Getting Killed

The release date for How to Write a Book without Getting Killed was August 27, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. This humorous RPG puts you in the role of an unemployed college graduate hired as an author's assistant, tasked with ensuring your eccentric boss, Akari, survives her hands-on research trips across five wildly different worlds.

You'll traverse settings from steampunk to medieval fantasy and even a space station, engaging in turn-based combat and solving puzzles while managing Akari's penchant for danger. With a choice between a male or female protagonist (Rito or Risa) that influences dialogue and romance, this adventure offers a personalized narrative. If you enjoy character-driven RPGs with lighthearted charm and diverse environments, this one is now available.
