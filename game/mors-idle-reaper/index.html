About Mors: Idle Reaper

The release date for Mors: Idle Reaper is July 23, 2026, on PC. This indie title mixes traditional idle progression with a high-stakes gambling loop that forces you to choose between cashing out your resources or risking them against difficult boss encounters.

Progression relies on managing seven distinct resources that generate even while offline, which you then funnel into gear upgrades and permanent rebirth multipliers. The stand-out feature is the Boss Ante system, where you can manually crank up the difficulty to Blood or Death tiers for a chance at mythic-tier loot. It is a game of calculated greed; dying during a hunt means losing your entire accumulated reward bank, making the decision to reset your run for skill points a constant tension. This is a perfect pick for those who want a hands-off experience that still demands strategic intervention during boss fights.

Fans of incremental games and loot-grinding should wishlist this for its risk-versus-reward mechanics.
