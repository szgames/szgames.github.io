About Karma Kingdom

The release date for Karma Kingdom is August 15, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This strategy RPG tasks you with restoring a world shattered by magical conflict, focusing on bringing land, people, and equipment from Netherspace to create a new home.

Uniquely, the game is heavily centered on themes of ecology, climate change, and the plight of refugees, drawing inspiration from its web version's charity links. As your kingdom expands and more people seek aid, you must manage resources and magic while considering the environment, citizen happiness, and acts of charity. Plan your rebuild carefully: this is a thoughtful purchase for strategy fans seeking a meaningful simulation.
