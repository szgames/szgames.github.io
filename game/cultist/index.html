About Cultist

The PC release date for Cultist is July 23, 2026. This turn-based card game focuses on the birth and evolution of ancient Mediterranean and Middle Eastern faiths, tasking players with transforming simple beliefs into immortal world religions.

Success hinges on managing cultists as a finite resource; every card flipped to establish a new religious doctrine permanently removes that follower from your ranks. This creates a high-stakes attrition loop where expansion directly threatens your survival, as losing your entire population results in immediate defeat. It is a strategic balancing act between aggressive growth and maintaining enough of a flock to withstand the influence of rival sects. Historical enthusiasts should watch this for its grounded anthropological approach to deckbuilding and theology.

Fans of strategic simulation should wishlist this for its unique focus on religious history.
