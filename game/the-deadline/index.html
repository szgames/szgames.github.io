About The DeadLine

The release date for The DeadLine is July 19, 2026, on PC. This third-person asymmetric horror game pits a single killer against a team of survivors in a high-stakes hunt. While survivors must scavenge for mechanical parts to repair their escape route, the killer relies on movement detection and psychological pressure to intercept them before they can flee the map.

The focus on item-based objectives rather than simple survival timers adds a layer of predictability that smart killers will exploit. Expect a heavy emphasis on map control and tracking mechanics as you decide between stealthy repairs or aggressive hunting. This is a solid choice for fans of competitive horror looking for a fresh strategic loop. Wishlist this if you want a new arena for team-based survival.
