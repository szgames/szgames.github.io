About Stella Echoes

The release date for Stella Echoes was July 23, 2026, launching exclusively for PC. Developed by digsteel, this indie puzzle title challenges you to navigate two characters across mirrored dimensions using a single set of movement inputs. While both heroes respond to the same commands, the horizontal directions are reversed in one world, turning every step into a calculated risk.

Success depends on using environmental obstacles like conveyors, walls, and one-way gates to desynchronize your movement and nudge characters into their respective goals. The difficulty lies in managing these precise spatial offsets across increasingly lethal stages where a single misstep in one realm can trap you in the other. It is a pure test of logic and planning for those who want their spatial reasoning pushed to the limit. Buy it now if you enjoy minimalist puzzle design that demands high execution.
