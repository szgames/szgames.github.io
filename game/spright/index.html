About Spright

The release date for Spright was August 21, 2026, bringing its challenging 2D Metroidvania action to PC. You descend into a vast abyss equipped with only a diving helm and longsword, but the deeper you go, the more the world twists and fights back, demanding sharp, aggressive, and movement-driven combat.

This isn't a simple hack-and-slash; enemies in Spright are designed to counter, reflect, or even grow stronger, making every encounter a tactical challenge. With five unique biomes and hidden paths to explore, players will uncover new tools to push deeper into the hostile environment. If you seek a true test of skill in a modern Metroidvania, you can now take a deep dive.
