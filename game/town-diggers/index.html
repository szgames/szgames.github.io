About Town Diggers

The release date for Town Diggers is September 2, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This casual simulation game tasks players with unearthing hidden items, running their own shop, and building wealth in a ruthless capitalist town. Players will rent digging areas to excavate valuable goods, then display them in their growing shop to sell through customer negotiation.

A unique twist lets you pay to hire workers for digging, allowing you to focus more on shop expansion, item management, and haggling for the best prices. This blend of relaxed discovery and business strategy aims to reward players who enjoy seeing their enterprise grow. Consider a wishlist if a blend of casual digging and shop management sounds appealing.
