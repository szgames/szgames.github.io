About DragonSword : Awakening

The release date for DragonSword: Awakening is July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by Hound13 Inc., this action RPG uses Unreal Engine 5 to render a bright, anime-style open world across the Continent of Orbis. You follow a young protagonist named Lute as he is swept into a mercenary life alongside an elf and a hired blade, eventually hunting a dragon that has returned after six decades of peace.

The combat focuses on tag-team mechanics and status ailment synergies, allowing you to cycle through a roster of companions and collectible Familiars to exploit enemy weaknesses. Outside of high-speed encounters, the loop involves survival-lite activities like cooking and exploring vertical environments ranging from deep-sea treasures to watchtower cellars. It looks to be a polished choice for players who want a high-fidelity aesthetic paired with character-swapping tactical combat. Wishlist this if you want a massive, vibrant fantasy world with tight party-based action.
