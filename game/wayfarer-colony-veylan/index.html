About Wayfarer Colony: Veylan

The release date for Wayfarer Colony: Veylan is July 29, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This action-exploration title casts players as Kael, a Wayfarer pilot charting the Veylan star system for humanity's new home, all while battling the rival Vesper Accord.

Its distinctive retro 3D visuals, reminiscent of early polygonal games with flat-shaded models, set it apart as you journey through 30 missions across diverse, hostile planets and moons. Expect a blend of intense combat and quiet exploration in vast environments designed for minimalism. Wishlist it if you appreciate a unique aesthetic paired with open-ended exploration and a sci-fi narrative.
