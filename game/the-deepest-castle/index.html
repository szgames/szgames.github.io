About THE DEEPEST CASTLE

The release date for THE DEEPEST CASTLE was July 23, 2026, for PC. This action RPG tasks you with infiltrating a forest fortress held by necromancers to recover hidden artifacts and rare weaponry. Combat revolves around tight melee timing, requiring you to learn enemy movesets and master shield parries to survive encounters with ancient warriors.

While the game uses a roguelite structure where death sends you back to the start, you retain all found weapons and artifacts between runs. This permanent progression mitigates the difficulty of its reflex-heavy sword fighting, allowing you to gradually shift your combat style as you uncover more of the castle's layout. Success relies on personal mechanical skill and the discovery of secret rooms that house the most powerful magic.

Pick this up if you want a punishing, melee-focused dungeon crawler with persistent loot.
