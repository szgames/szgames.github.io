About GUTTED: Infested Crypts

The release date for GUTTED: Infested Crypts was August 17, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This action-horror FPS throws players into a methodical descent through crypts infested by rotting horrors, armed with heavy gunplay and brutal dismemberment as the primary tools for survival.

Success depends on navigating suffocating environments where visibility is scarce, illuminated only by a dying flashlight. Every skittering sound and distant screech hints at danger lurking just beyond the light, making careful progression crucial. Check out this one if you crave intense, close-quarters horror with a strong emphasis on atmosphere and sound design.
