About Dice & Dare : A Push-Your-Luck Casino-Style Dice Roguelike

The release date for Dice & Dare : A Push-Your-Luck Casino-Style Dice Roguelike was July 24, 2026, on PC. This indie title moves away from traditional combat-focused deckbuilders to focus entirely on the tension of a rising pot and the probability of a bust.

Success relies on managing a synergy board where you stack charms, relics, and jokers to modify your rolls and create massive scoring cascades. The loop is a pure test of greed, forcing you to decide between banking a safe score or risking everything on a single roll to trigger complex modifiers. It is a high-speed engine builder designed for those who want the thrill of a casino floor mixed with the strategic depth of a modern roguelike.

Grab this for your library if you want a calculated, numbers-driven gamble that rewards aggressive play and smart relic stacking.
