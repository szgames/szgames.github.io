About Frontline: Operation Kosmonaut

The release date for Frontline: Operation Kosmonaut was July 23, 2026, on PC. Set in 1987, this tactical action title casts you as a Spetsnaz operative tasked with retaking a seized orbital facility from rogue Soviet military officers. The mission focuses on internal sabotage and deniable operations within frozen northern territories, requiring you to clear underground bunkers and rocket hangars to secure the Kosmonaut program.

This is a deliberate callback to the era of late-90s military shooters, stripping away modern conveniences like health regeneration and open-world bloat. Success depends on resource management and navigating close-quarters combat with limited medkits and a minimal HUD. By prioritizing a linear, grounded campaign over current trends, it targets a specific demographic that prefers high-stakes lethality and 1980s Cold War aesthetics. Fans of classic, unforgiving shooters can now play this following its July launch.
