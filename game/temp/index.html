About temp

The release date for temp is August 13, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This 2.5D psychological mystery casts players as a boy investigating disappearances in his neighborhood, not through combat, but by meticulously studying and recreating the events himself. It blends cinematic side-scrolling exploration with emergent systems, challenging you to shape outcomes through careful observation and deduction.

temp features a unique psychological sandbox puzzle design where you uncover patterns and react to subtle environmental changes rather than fighting enemies. The quest for truth becomes uncertain and personal, blurring lines between memory and imagination in a modern, culturally grounded setting. If a deep, non-linear mystery driven by quiet tension and player deduction appeals, mark this down for a purchase.
