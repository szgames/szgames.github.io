About To Home

The release date for To Home is July 21, 2026, on PC. This 3D precision platformer casts you as a tiny robot navigating a vertical graveyard of floating cosmic debris to find a path back to earth.

The challenge lies in the absolute lack of checkpoints, where a single mistimed jump sends you tumbling back to the very beginning of the level. Success depends entirely on steady nerves and mastering the robot's momentum during tight platforming sequences. It is a focused, high-stakes experience designed for those who find satisfaction in punishing mechanical mastery and the tension of a potential long fall.

Buy this if you want a testing vertical climb where every movement carries real consequence.
