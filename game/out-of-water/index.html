About Out Of Water

The release date for Out Of Water was September 1, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This visual novel plunges players into 1 CE Watarholt, a northern Germanic village grappling with mysterious surfacing bodies and a fading peace. The game focuses on daily village life slowly unraveling into danger and grief, where your choices dictate conversations, relationships, and how the community responds to fear.

What makes this notable is the blend of grounded historical setting with ancient horrors, emphasizing the weight of player decisions in a world turning hostile. The inclusion of a free 45-page artbook with lore and development insights further enriches the experience for fans of narrative depth. If you seek a choice-driven visual novel blending historical mystery and cosmic dread, you can now play this for its intriguing premise and included extras.
