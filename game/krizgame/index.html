About krizgame

The release date for krizgame is August 25, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This indie adventure challenges players with an extreme permadeath rule: you only get one chance to complete the game. Once you fail or finish, you cannot play again.

To win, you must conquer five distinct puzzles, each with its own mechanics, within a 15-minute time limit. Each attempt offers a personalized environment around the consistent puzzles, adding a layer of unpredictable visual flair to the high-stakes run. For players seeking an intense, single-attempt puzzle experience, krizgame is a definitive purchase on release.
