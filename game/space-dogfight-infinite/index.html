About Space Dogfight: Infinite

The release date for Space Dogfight: Infinite is July 23, 2026, on PC. This technical arcade title strips away the progression systems of modern roguelikes to focus entirely on physics-based combat in a cold, pixelated vacuum. Without health bars or character buffs, success depends on your ability to manage momentum and drift through zero-gravity environments while lining up lethal shots against AI fighters and massive warships.

The core challenge lies in the inertia-driven movement where every engine thrust must be calculated against the lack of air resistance. While you can unlock different engines and weapon types to adjust your handling, the game remains a pure test of prediction and reaction rather than statistical power. It is a specialized experience built for those who find satisfaction in the steep learning curve of Newtonian physics and precision ballistics. Buy this if you want a demanding, skill-first space sim that rewards mastery over luck.
