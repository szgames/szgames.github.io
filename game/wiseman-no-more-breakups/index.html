About Wiseman: No More Breakups

The release date for Wiseman: No More Breakups is July 20, 2026, for PC. This title is a high-stakes rhythm-combat boss rush where August Wiseman must fight his way through a moving train filled with his ex-girlfriend's family and social circle to retrieve his phone.

The mechanics focus on four-button inputs synced to a jazz soundtrack, where every successful beat triggers an attack and every mistake results in taking damage. The game enforces a strict permadeath rule for each run, meaning a single failure resets your progress across every boss and train car. It is a stylish arcade challenge that relies entirely on execution and pattern recognition rather than traditional RPG leveling. Buy this if you want a punishing, high-energy rhythm experience with no checkpoints.
