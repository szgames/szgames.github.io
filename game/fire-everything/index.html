About Fire Everything!

The release date for Fire Everything! was August 27, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This is a space bullet heaven where survival against endless alien swarms hinges on acquiring absurdly overpowered builds. It immediately sets itself apart by focusing on strategic upgrade drafting and unique ship supers, transforming your single weapon into a screen-clearing menace.

Every level-up offers choices to chain lightning, make crits explode, or drop black holes, encouraging broken combo discovery. Players can charge a unique "FIRE EVERYTHING" super on each ship, unleashing massive attacks at critical moments, across Survival, Campaign, and Daily Challenges. Expect to die often, then spend salvage on new ships, weapons, and permanent power-ups for the next run. This is a buy for bullet heaven fanatics ready to break the game.
