About Frogrammer

The release date for Frogrammer was July 20, 2026, and it is now available for PC. This strategy title blends resource management with a tug-of-war auto-battler structure where you recruit specialized amphibian units to clear out an encroaching insect horde.

Success depends on balancing your budget against the escalating salary demands of high-tier units, ranging from small tadpoles to massive juggernauts. Since every unit features a mix of innate and randomized skills, you are constantly weighing the cost of a hire against the specific synergies needed to counter current enemy waves. The core challenge lies in efficient positioning and exploiting elemental openings while keeping your overhead low enough to survive the long push toward the source of the infestation. It is a focused tactical experiment for those who prefer managing a lean, high-performing squad over simple unit spamming.

Check this out if you want a tight, cost-conscious take on auto-battler positioning.
