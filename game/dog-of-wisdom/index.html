About Dog of Wisdom

The release date for Dog of Wisdom is July 23, 2026, for PC. This casual side-scroller features Pepper the dachshund on a flight through the galaxy to collect safe snacks and unlock cosmic insights. Developed in GB Studio and ported for modern systems, it recreates the aesthetic and mechanical limitations of handheld retro hardware.

The gameplay revolves around a simple educational hook where you must distinguish between dog-safe treats and toxic foods while navigating various environments. It is a light, straightforward experience designed for quick sessions rather than complex systems or deep narrative. This title serves as a playful interactive companion to the internet meme of the same name and fits best for those looking for a short, retro-styled distraction. Purchase it if you want a quirky, low-stakes arcade game with a specific focus on dog-based humor.
