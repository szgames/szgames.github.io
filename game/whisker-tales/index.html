About Whisker Tales

The release date for Whisker Tales is July 19, 2026, on PC. Developed by Buya Studios, this idle game centers on a colony of cats tasked with restoring an ancient library by gathering knowledge crumbs. While the early loop focuses on clicking and simple automation, the inclusion of a specialized Sakura Room introduces a gacha-style chest system to recruit legendary cats for automated battles.

The title shifts from a gray-scale world to a colorful one as you unlock the skill tree and upgrade feline stats like attack speed and health regeneration. It functions as a low-pressure management title where progress continues while offline, making it a fit for those who want a hands-off experience with a collection layer. This is one to wishlist for players who want a cozy aesthetic paired with light RPG team building.
