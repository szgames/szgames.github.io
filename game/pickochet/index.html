About Pickochet

The release date for Pickochet was July 22, 2026, and it is available on PC. This casual simulation focuses on a short, focused incremental loop where mining is handled by pickaxes that ricochet off walls and ore deposits to generate passive income.

The central hook relies on automating the grind. While you start by clicking manually, the strategy shifts toward managing up to three active or passive abilities and balancing upgrades across multiple mining sites. It is built as a compact experience rather than an endless treadmill, making it a good fit if you want a clear beginning and end to your progression systems. Pick up this title if you want a low-friction idle game with a physics-based twist.
