About Sir, We Have an Orc Problem

The release date for Sir, We Have an Orc Problem was July 28, 2026, and it is now available on PC. This strategy title shifts the scale of traditional tower defense by pitting you against tens of thousands of individual units simultaneously, forcing a reliance on massive area-of-effect weaponry and a deep meta-progression tree to survive the sheer volume of the horde.

Success depends on balancing high-output turrets with active abilities like strafing runs and nukes to clear the screen when the lane density becomes unmanageable. It is an incremental experience where every failed run feeds back into permanent upgrades, making it a strong fit for those who enjoy the power scaling of survivors-likes applied to a stationary base defense format. Buy this if you want to test your hardware and your strategy against overwhelming enemy counts.
