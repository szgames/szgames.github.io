About Scary Stories: Grotesque

The release date for Scary Stories: Grotesque is July 23, 2026, on PC. Developed by Game Museum, this title is a curated collection of short-form horror visual novels that take the mundane and twist it into something unrecognizable. Rather than relying on grand fantasies, the narrative focus stays locked on the unsettling reality of late-night commutes, empty cinemas, and lonely bedrooms.

What sets this apart is its commitment to the unresolved nature of modern folklore. Many of these vignettes intentionally leave the protagonist and the player without answers, mirroring the sudden, inexplicable encounters typical of internet ghost stories. This isn't a single continuous arc but a growing anthology that the developers plan to expand with more urban legends over time. It is a precise pick for players who prefer the psychological dread of a well-told creepy-pasta over high-action jump scares.

Check this out if you want a series of bite-sized, unsettling vignettes to play through in one sitting.
