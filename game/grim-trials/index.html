About Grim Trials

The release date for Grim Trials was August 20, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

This heavy metal action roguelite blends weapon crafting and dual-wielding combat with a narrative focused on an aspiring Grim Reaper navigating the afterlife to reunite with her still-living love. Players will hack and blast through endless hex-grid arenas, customizing scythes and crossbows while gaining powerful Blessings from Death Gods. The combat system rewards clever use of both weapons, and resources gathered in battle fuel extensive crafting and a deep skill tree for each run. If you crave a challenging action RPG with deep customization, you can now purchase Grim Trials.
