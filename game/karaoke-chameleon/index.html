About Karaoke Chameleon

The release date for Karaoke Chameleon was July 31, 2026, and it is now available for PC. This indie title twists the standard multiplayer hide-and-seek formula by forcing hidden players to disguise themselves as musical instruments. To stay hidden, ghosts must perform convincing, rhythmic melodies on drums, pianos, or trumpets, while hunters listen for the erratic button-mashing that reveals a fake.

Success depends entirely on your ability to maintain a steady beat under pressure. Hunters operate with a strict ammo count, meaning every missed shot based on a musical hunch brings the ghosts closer to victory. It is a high-stakes test of rhythm where sounding like a pro is the only way to survive the round. Grab this if you want a social deduction game that rewards a good ear and steady timing.
