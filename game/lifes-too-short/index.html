About Life's Too Short

The release date for Life's Too Short is July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by Pixel Ghost, this adventure follows a celestial ghost summoned to a mansion to resolve a bitter feud between two vampires who have retreated into their coffins. Rather than focusing on scares, the experience is built around positive interactions and light problem-solving within a supernatural setting.

The central loop involves helping the mansion's eccentric residents, including ghouls and cats, to restore order and improve the atmosphere of the house. It is a low-stakes puzzle game that emphasizes charm and conflict resolution over traditional horror tropes. This is a clear choice for players who want a cozy, bite-sized mystery with a lighthearted tone.

Wishlist this if you want a short, stress-free adventure about being a helpful spirit.
