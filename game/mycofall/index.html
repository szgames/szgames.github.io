About Mycofall

The release date for Mycofall is July 21, 2026, for PC. This survivors-like auto-shooter shifts the focus from simple survival to a specialized resource loop where killing enemies directly triggers the growth of mycelium, the currency required for evolution. Unlike many entries in the genre that prioritize static positioning, success here relies on high-speed pathing to harvest these mushrooms before the swarm closes in.

The inclusion of four-player online co-op sets it apart from the typical solo bullet-heaven experience, allowing teams to coordinate build decisions and share progress across the leaderboard. The core loop demands a balance between min-maxing your mutation tree and managing a respawn meter that dictates how frequently resources appear on the map. It is a technical take on the genre that rewards efficient movement and aggressive clearing over passive play.

Grab this if you want a cooperative bullet-hell with deep build optimization and high-pressure resource management.
