About Random Isles

The release date for Random Isles is July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by Mounight, this title blends RPG progression with a strategic card-drawing system where your survival depends on the luck of the pack. You spend currency on five tiers of card packs to unlock nearly 200 different items and skills, ranging from common white gear to elusive silver-tier equipment found only in the rarest red packs.

The loop centers on using map cards to generate random stages. These instances vary between high-stakes combat encounters and peaceful reward zones filled with chests. Unlike standard deckbuilders where cards are purely for combat, here they act as your primary inventory management and world-generation tool. The progression is defined by how effectively you can cycle through lower-tier packs to find the orange and red cards needed to challenge the game's toughest enemies. Fans of loot-heavy inventory management and RNG-based character building should wishlist this for its July launch.
