About MoonScape

The release date for MoonScape is July 24, 2026, launching on PC. This simulation and strategy hybrid places you in charge of a lunar mining operation that remains active even after you log off. While your base extracts resources and auto-repairs damage in real time, you must manage a persistent war room to produce units and research upgrades to survive the corporate land grab.

The primary hook is the intelligence gathering mechanic, where your radar arrays allow you to profile rival bases to weigh potential losses against spoils before committing to a raid. Because the world never pauses, you will return to the game to find the results of automated defenses and completed research cycles. The mystery of the moon's previous inhabitants adds a layer of discovery to the constant threat of corporate sabotage. Strategy fans looking for a competitive management game with persistent stakes should wishlist this for 2026.
