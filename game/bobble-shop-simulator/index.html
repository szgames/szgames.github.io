About Bobble Shop Simulator

The release date for Bobble Shop Simulator was July 23, 2026, on PC. Taking over a family-run business, you are tasked with transforming a small storefront into a destination for collectors through a mix of inventory management, store customization, and aggressive advertising.

While the loop focuses on the familiar tasks of stocking shelves and hiring staff, the game distinguishes itself with a repair system for damaged goods and a hint of the supernatural. The rarest collectibles, known as Agree-A-Bobbles, reportedly possess a mind of their own, adding an unpredictable element to your display cases that could impact store reviews. You will need to balance the hunt for rare inventory with the practical costs of painting walls and expanding your floor space.

Management fans looking for a niche retail hook should check out this for its unique focus on collectible trading.
