About The Relic: First Guardian

The release date for The Relic: First Guardian was July 31, 2026, for PC. This semi-open world action RPG tasks players with repairing a shattered reality by recovering fragments of a destroyed artifact in a land consumed by an encroaching void.

Rather than relying on traditional experience points, the game uses a rune-based progression system where character power is defined by crafting and itemization. You have five distinct weapon classes to master, each featuring modular skills that can be swapped to fit specific boss encounters or exploration needs. The lack of standard leveling places a heavy emphasis on dungeon mastery and careful resource management to survive the apocalyptic setting. Success depends on your ability to adapt your gear and runic setup to the lethal threats found within the world's various ruins.

Those looking for a skill-driven RPG that prioritizes gear synergy over grinding can now play this title.
