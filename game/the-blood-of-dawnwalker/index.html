About The Blood of Dawnwalker

The release date for The Blood of Dawnwalker is September 2, 2026, launching on PC, PlayStation, and Xbox. Developed by Rebel Wolves, this dark fantasy action RPG drops you into a grim 14th-century Europe ravaged by the Black Death and a sudden vampire uprising. You play as Coen, a Dawnwalker forced to balance human morals with supernatural predatory instincts to protect your family from the chaos.

The central hook is the daylight-sensitive mechanic that shifts your capabilities based on the sun's position, requiring careful management of your dual nature. Unlike high-fantasy epics, this focuses on a grounded, historical atmosphere where folklore and plague collide. You will need to decide whether to lean into your vampiric powers at the cost of your humanity or stay tethered to your mortal roots through difficult narrative choices. Purchase this if you want a gritty, narrative-driven RPG with high-stakes character progression.
