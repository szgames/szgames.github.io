About Cosdarica's Journey at the End of the World

The release date for Cosdarica's Journey at the End of the World was July 27, 2026, on PC. While most of humanity has retreated to safe zones to await the inevitable, this title follows two individuals who choose to wander the remains of the world instead.

This is a focused, short-form visual novel designed to be read in a single sitting of twenty to forty minutes. It strips away branching paths and complex mechanics to deliver a singular, linear narrative accompanied by ten original illustrations. The value here lies in its atmospheric finality, serving as a brief digital novella about finding meaning in the final moments of a dying civilization.

Pick this up for a quick, contemplative story during a quiet evening.
