About Incremental Infinity

The release date for Incremental Infinity is July 20, 2026, on PC. Developed by Rablo Games, this abstract strategy title focuses on exponential growth through a procedurally generated skill tree that literally never ends. You manage a numeric value that represents both your health and your physical size, firing automatically while you navigate a web of upgrades to keep pace with scaling enemy waves.

The central hook is a high-stakes loop where a single defeat resets your current run back to one, though you retain permanent upgrades and ascension bonuses to accelerate the next attempt. Because the game functions well as a side-window companion, it targets players who want a persistent sense of progression while multitasking. It is a pure numbers game that replaces traditional combat with the satisfaction of watching an infinite build evolve. Buy this if you want a strategic idle experience that rewards long-term optimization.
