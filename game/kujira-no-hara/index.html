About Kujira no Hara

The release date for Kujira no Hara is July 21, 2026, for PC. This adventure places a child inside the belly of a massive whale, establishing a self-contained world where a society of Vikings and eccentric castaways have formed a strange new home. It follows a classic top-down action template with a heavy focus on gaining new tools to unlock previously inaccessible sections of the organism.

While it draws clear inspiration from handheld Zelda classics, this title leans much harder into logical friction. With seven primary dungeons and a projected 50-hour runtime, the difficulty of the environmental puzzles is intentionally tuned higher than its inspirations to challenge genre veterans. The sheer volume of side content, including over 100 optional quests, suggests a dense experience for those who value thorough exploration and unconventional NPC interactions. Buy this if you want a lengthy, high-difficulty puzzle adventure with a bizarre sense of humor.
