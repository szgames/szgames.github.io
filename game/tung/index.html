About TUNG

The release date for TUNG was August 6, 2026, and the game is now available exclusively on PC.

This Niche Games title challenges players to survive a seemingly ordinary hedge maze that quickly devolves into a terrifying trap. You'll enter on a dare, armed only with a flashlight, but soon discover signs of others who never escaped, before you're hunted by the mysterious TUNG. If atmospheric first-person horror and getting lost in a deadly labyrinth appeal, this is definitely one to check out on PC.
