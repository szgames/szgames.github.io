About TAOLU

The release date for TAOLU on PC was July 23, 2026, and the game became available on July 24, 2026. This martial arts simulation focuses entirely on the traditional wooden dummy training used in disciplines like Wing Chun, Hung Gar, and Shaolin, bringing centuries-old practice into a virtual and mixed reality space.

The game distinguishes itself by prioritizing form and precision over simple arcade striking. With five distinct modes including survival and a specialized Wing Chun sequence modeled after Ip Man's techniques, it functions more as a technical trainer than a standard action title. The inclusion of a free mode and global rankings suggests a loop built around daily practice and incremental skill improvement. If you have ever wanted a Wing Chun dummy but lacked the physical space, this is the most direct digital equivalent available.

Martial arts enthusiasts and VR fitness users can now get this for a disciplined training tool.
