About Arlo Part I: The Past

The release date for Arlo Part I: The Past was July 29, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This first-person supernatural psychological horror game drops you into Arlo's mind as she confronts ancient demons, with no hand-holding or on-screen objectives; success demands a sharp detective mindset.

Players will face entities like Bifrons, a fallen angel turned demon, all pulled from real demonological lore found in ancient texts and occult tradition. This commitment to authentic horror deepens the terror, as these creatures have histories predating the game by centuries. If you crave a psychological horror that asks you to truly investigate and are drawn to lore-rich supernatural threats, play Arlo Part I for its intense, no-guides approach.
