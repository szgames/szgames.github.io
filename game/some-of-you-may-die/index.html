About Some of You May Die

The release date for Some of You May Die was July 24, 2026, and it is now available on PC. This strategic autobattler focuses on breaking the typical balance of the genre by allowing you to manually invest gold into specific hero stat sheets. Rather than relying on rigid character archetypes, you can force a priest into a high-armor tank role or build an assassin specifically to bypass the enemy frontline based on the unique ability rolls each unit receives.

The game features a single-player campaign across seven distinct squads and five difficulty tiers, but the real draw is the real-time online PvP. Because each match pulls from a shifting pool of upgrades, success depends on identifying high-synergy combinations that counter your opponent's specific build before they do the same to you. Strategy fans who enjoy pushing systems to their absolute mechanical limits should check this one out. Buy this if you want an autobattler that prioritizes theorycrafting and extreme stat customization over simple unit placement.
