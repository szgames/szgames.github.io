About Soulbound: Online

The release date for Soulbound: Online was July 21, 2026, on PC. Developed by SpiderWare, this title attempts a rare hybrid by layering survivor-style bullet-heaven combat into a persistent MMORPG framework. While you spend your time in a shared overworld farming, crafting, and trading with thousands of other players, the core action happens in instanced roguelite dungeons designed for solo play or three-player co-op.

The draw here is the synergy system, featuring over 100 combinations of abilities and relics that can completely alter a class build during a single run. Balancing a massive multiplayer economy with the randomized power spikes of a roguelite is a difficult design challenge, but the addition of personal base building and large-scale raids gives the loop a clear sense of long-term progression. Fans of top-down action looking for a social experience with high-speed combat should check this out.

This is a strong pick for players who want the mechanical chaos of a roguelite without losing the social features of an MMO.
