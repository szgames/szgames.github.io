About Foodie Tour

The release date for Foodie Tour on PC is July 22, 2026. This casual puzzle game moves away from standard square grids, using a hexagonal layout where you match specific food items to satisfy four distinct types of customers, from picky eaters to high-volume big eaters.

Success depends on time management rather than move counts. You clear the board by matching the specific dishes requested by the current diner, while matching unneeded items charges powerful boosters like the Bussing Cart or the Rainbow Plate. With over 320 levels across sixteen international themes and a built-in scene editor for sharing custom stages, there is a significant amount of content for a puzzle title. The inclusion of three difficulty tiers and a combo system suggests a higher skill ceiling than typical match-three games.

Puzzle fans looking for a speed-focused challenge should wishlist this for the July launch.
