About Sweet Land Survival

The release date for Sweet Land Survival on PC is July 22, 2026. This indie title mixes traditional survival mechanics with a sugary aesthetic, putting you in control of a bear stranded on a candy island where every action is tied to a strict hunger system.

While the visuals are bright, the management layer is punishing; failing to maintain your energy levels via fishing and jam collection directly slows your movement and weakens your combat effectiveness. You operate as a trio rather than a solo survivor, directing two AI bear companions to divide the labor of resource gathering and fighting off the island's native monsters. The success of your run depends entirely on how efficiently you juggle these team roles while scavenging for edible materials. This is one for survival fans who want a tighter focus on squad management and stat maintenance over traditional base building. Keep this on your radar if you want a distinctive spin on resource-based progression.
