About Pop Star Life

The release date for Pop Star Life was August 14, 2026, and the game launched exclusively on PC.

This single-player, text-based sandbox simulation offers a uniquely granular look at building a music career, forcing players to balance songwriting and performing with the real-world demands of managing finances, health, stress, and relationships. Every choice, from street performances to studio sessions, directly shapes your artist's long-term journey to fame. If a deeply analytical, text-driven ascent to stardom appeals, be sure to check this one out.
