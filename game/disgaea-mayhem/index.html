About Disgaea Mayhem

The release date for Disgaea Mayhem on PC was July 23, 2026. This title shifts the long-running strategy series into a real-time action RPG where you take direct control of a mercenary protecting a dessert-obsessed princess.

While the perspective has changed, the series' signature obsession with massive numbers remains intact. You can expect to grind levels and dive into the Item World to boost gear stats until your damage output hits the millions. The core hook is the seven distinct weapon classes that allow you to swap between melee and ranged combat on the fly, supported by a Dark Chocolate Assembly for passing character-buffing bills. It is a faster, more aggressive way to experience the franchise without losing the familiar recruitment of classic demons and Prinnies.

Buy this if you want the Disgaea grind and humor in a hack-and-slash format.
