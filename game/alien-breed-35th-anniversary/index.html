About Alien Breed 35th Anniversary

The release date for Alien Breed 35th Anniversary was September 23, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This collection revives the classic isometric sci-fi horror-action series, bringing back the first era of games optimized for modern systems. The game features overhauled optical tools, modern combat control augments, and new software enhancements for smoother play.

Infiltrate a blacked-out station, fight xenomorphs, and uncover classified secrets across six original campaigns. The game supports solo play, local co-op, or peer-to-peer online multiplayer, alongside an all-new Horde Mode for training and survival. Fans of retro sci-fi horror looking for a modern refresh of a classic should consider this a definite purchase for their PC.
