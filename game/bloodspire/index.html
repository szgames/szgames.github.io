About BloodSpire

The release date for BloodSpire is July 23, 2026, for PC. This fast-paced action roguelike puts you in control of a cat fighting through a monster-filled castle tower using a mix of physical weapons and magic abilities.

While the premise is whimsical, the mechanical depth comes from a dense upgrade system involving card-based power-ups and specific weapon damage attributes. You can build out your feline warrior with defensive orb shields, ice projectiles, or elemental flames while managing classic platforming movement like wall jumps and dashes. The inclusion of a dedicated speedrunner mode and mini-games suggests a focus on replayability and high-skill execution rather than just casual survival.

Anyone looking for a high-energy platformer with meaningful character builds should wishlist this one.
