About ZAVOD: Conveyor Logic

The release date for ZAVOD: Conveyor Logic was July 24, 2026, on PC. This minimalist puzzle title tasks you with configuring conveyor belts across fifty levels to assist struggling coworkers before their shift ends.

Success depends on managing factory flow while dealing with coworkers who occasionally interfere with your progress. The tension comes from the strict supervision of an ever-present boss, forcing you to solve spatial logic problems under a constant watchful eye. While the controls are straightforward, the challenge lies in the specific mechanical quirks introduced as you advance through the production line.

Pick this up if you want a clean, focused automation puzzler that rewards efficient layout design.
