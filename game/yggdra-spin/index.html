About YGGDRA: SPIN

The release date for YGGDRA: SPIN is July 20, 2026, and the game will be available on PC. This title blends RPG progression with a slot-battler roguelite system where your currency and health are one and the same.

Success in this cursed world depends on building a deck of 33 supernatural heroines and various items that function as symbols on your reels. While combat resolves automatically once you pull the lever, the depth lies in managing factions and positions to trigger explosive damage multipliers. The critical risk here is the financial management aspect; every spin costs funds, and running out of money means an immediate game over. It is a gamble on synergy where a single lucky rotation can save a failing run.

Strategic players who enjoy high-risk resource management should wishlist this one for its launch.
