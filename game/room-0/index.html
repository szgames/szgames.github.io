About Room 0

The release date for Room 0 was July 29, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This first-person horror game puts you in the shoes of a detective sent to an abandoned research facility to uncover the truth behind a missing girl named Carla. The investigation centers on a sealed room and the chilling incident that led to the facility's closure.

As you explore, your only tools are a vintage VHS camcorder and the traces Carla left behind, offering glimpses into a lethal present where an unseen presence still lurks. The game delivers a concentrated atmospheric experience focused on environmental storytelling and psychological tension. If you're seeking a focused horror game about uncovering dark secrets, get Room 0 today.
