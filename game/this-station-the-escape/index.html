About This Station: The Escape

The release date for This Station: The Escape is July 23, 2026, on PC. Developed by DMONG Co., Ltd., this adventure title drops you into a dark, abandoned subway terminal after you miss your stop on the last train, forcing you to navigate a secret laboratory to prevent a looming disaster.

This is a deliberate, logic-heavy experience that prioritizes observation over reflexes. By removing combat and time limits, the game focuses entirely on environmental storytelling and item-based puzzle solving within a quiet, high-stakes atmosphere. Your ability to connect everyday objects to a larger terrorist conspiracy determines which ending you unlock. It is a focused choice for players who prefer methodical deduction and a slow-burning mystery over action-oriented escape rooms.

Pick this up on launch day if you want a pure test of reasoning.
