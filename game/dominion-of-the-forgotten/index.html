About Dominion Of The Forgotten

The release date for Dominion Of The Forgotten is July 19, 2026, for PC. This roguelike adventure tasks you with entering a cursed eastern continent to scavenge for artifacts and ancient powers within a strict time limit.

The gameplay focuses on extraction-style loops where build-crafting is the primary survival mechanic. You can customize your combat style using gear from different factions, like the Shark Tribe or Yuheng Clan, to handle threats solo or in a three-player cooperative squad. The tension centers on balancing your greed for relics against the ticking clock of the eldritch mist, as failing to reach an extraction point means losing your progress. Grab this if you want a team-based extraction RPG with deep equipment customization.
