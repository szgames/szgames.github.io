About The Loomsong Chronicle Idle

The release date for The Loomsong Chronicle Idle was August 18, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. This idle RPG from Nitrocodon Dynamics infuses the genre with the depth and progression of an ARPG, letting players hunt for massive loot, craft spells, and develop skill-based characters without level caps.

It sets its action in the unique fantasy/sci-fi "Galaxy of the Glittersong," where ancient beings and magic reshape worlds. Players can customize parties from 8 races and leverage 27 skills and 29 combinable abilities across 8 auto-explorable maps and four pinnacle boss fights. This is for players who want deep character progression and persistent loot chase in a hands-off format. Pick it up now for a chill ARPG experience.
