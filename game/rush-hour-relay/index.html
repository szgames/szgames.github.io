About Rush Hour Relay

The release date for Rush Hour Relay was July 28, 2026, and it launched on PC. This real-time strategy simulation challenges you to rebuild your grandfather's struggling delivery company, managing everything from stock acquisition to driver routes, all while fending off Sterling, a rival determined to take over your family's legacy.

Success hinges on a daily three-part logistics scramble: optimizing night stock purchases, meticulously loading drivers each morning to balance cargo weight against speed and fuel, and then adapting to live, timed orders across the city while competing directly against an aggressive AI. Every decision, from a driver's loadout to their detours for fuel, directly impacts your survival against corporate chains. Fans of intricate logistics puzzles and economic warfare can now purchase this game.
