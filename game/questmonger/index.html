About Questmonger

The release date for Questmonger is August 14, 2026, launching on PC.

This simulation puts you in charge of a crumbling adventurer's guild, where you match heroes to dangerous jobs and decide what gear to lend them, knowing every choice affects their survival and your bottom line. Heroes are permanent, gaining experience and loyalty, but one wrong assignment means they are gone for good, along with anything you invested. Beyond just matching skills, you must weigh hero costs against potential rewards, all while dealing with rent and a watchful royal inspector. Add Questmonger to your wishlist for a fresh take on fantasy management with high stakes and real consequences.
