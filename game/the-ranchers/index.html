About The Ranchers

The release date for The Ranchers on PC was July 30, 2026. This open-world country-life sim invites 1-4 players to escape the city and cultivate their dream farm, blending the relaxing pace of raising animals and growing crops with the freedom of a sandbox environment where you can build your house, expand your land, and explore a large world.

More than just a cozy farming experience, The Ranchers emphasizes earning respect from villagers and offers enough action to keep things engaging while providing creative freedom in base-building. Those looking for a deep, multiplayer farm sim should certainly check this one out.
