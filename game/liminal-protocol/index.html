About Liminal Protocol

The release date for Liminal Protocol is July 23, 2026, on PC. Developed by Rabbit House Games, this tactical action title drops up to four players into a facility that has been physically transformed into an unstable 3D ASCII lattice. You are tasked with synchronizing nodes and extracting evidence while the environment literally rewrites itself around your team.

The game relies on a striking visual style where monospaced coordinates and steel signals form the world, creating a high-tension atmosphere as interdimensional entities enter through spatial tears. Success depends on maintaining your neural link and managing resources while navigating corridors that no longer follow conventional physics. It avoids the typical horror tropes by focusing on the technical dread of a reality-warping containment failure. This is a strong choice for players who want a distinct aesthetic paired with coordinated, session-based extraction gameplay. Wishlist this if you prioritize atmosphere and unique art direction in your co-op shooters.
