About Molluscarium

The release date for Molluscarium is August 7, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This unique idle simulation offers the chance to collect over 500 mollusc species and transform them into interactive desktop companions that glide transparently over your work or browser windows.

Beyond simple collection, watch larvae evolve into mature snails and cephalopods in real-time, nurturing them across nine richly themed aquatic habitats. Unlock rare species and striking visual variants to build your ultimate living mollusc library. Fans of relaxing, low-interaction virtual pet games with a unique visual twist should absolutely wishlist this.
