About Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game

The release date for Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game was July 23, 2026, on PC. Developed by Gameplay Group International, this title brings the Four Nations into a competitive 1v1 setting using hand-drawn 2D animation that mirrors the original series' aesthetic.

The roster features 12 playable fighters, including series icons like Zuko, Toph, and Katara, each utilizing distinct bending styles to control space or apply pressure. High-stakes competitive play is the focus here, supported by proprietary rollback netcode to ensure frame-perfect elemental clashes. Beyond the standard arcade and versus modes, the inclusion of a story mode and a digital art gallery suggests a package designed to satisfy both technical fighting fans and series historians. Pre-ordering even allowed for a vote on the final Year 1 Pass character, giving the community direct influence over the post-launch meta.

Fighting game enthusiasts and fans of the franchise can now pick this up for a summer purchase.
