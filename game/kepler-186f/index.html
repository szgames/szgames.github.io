About Kepler 186F

The release date for Kepler 186F is July 22, 2026, on PC. This narrative-driven sci-fi thriller puts you in control of a Type-37 Crisis Response Robot tasked with investigating a silent colony on the frozen, dark side of a distant world.

You will focus on environmental problem-solving rather than combat, using circuit reconfiguration and energy waveform matching to navigate sub-zero temperatures and locked facilities. Success depends on your ability to sort through timeline logs and black boxes to piece together why the colony vanished. This is a technical adventure aimed at players who prefer methodical investigation and heavy puzzle-solving over fast-paced action. It is a solid choice for those who want a quiet, atmospheric mystery to unravel. Grab it if you enjoy cold, lonely science fiction with a focus on logic puzzles.
