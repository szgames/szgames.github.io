About Rabbit Comes Home

The release date for Rabbit Comes Home was July 28, 2026, when it launched exclusively on PC. This action-adventure title puts players in the role of a lone rabbit, stranded in outer space, on a desperate quest to return home. You'll need to defend your ship against relentless alien swarms while managing critical energy levels and constantly harvesting resources to survive.

The constant pressure of an energy deficit, combined with the need for continuous resource harvesting and strategic ship upgrades, distinguishes this roguelike survival challenge. Every warp jump brings you closer to your goal but also to new dangers, making each decision impactful. Prepare for a fight to return home; this is a clear recommendation for players who enjoy intense, resource-driven survival action.
