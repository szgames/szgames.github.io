About Well Dweller

The release date for Well Dweller is September 21, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This dark fairy-tale metroidvania from the creators of Crypt Custodian and Islets puts players in the tiny wings of Glimmer, a bird armed with a matchstick. You must brave a corrupted kingdom to burn the wicked queen and save your family.

Explore a rotting kingdom filled with terrifying beasts, using your matchstick to illuminate paths and aid in combat. Discover secrets, solve puzzles, and meet unique allies in the kingdom's underbelly while collecting trinkets to customize Glimmer's abilities. Players seeking an atmospheric metroidvania with a distinctive combat twist should definitely wishlist this one.
