About Cannon Clicker

The release date for Cannon Clicker was August 19, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC.

This unique idle clicker title takes players from humble beginnings to military supremacy in a World War-inspired setting, demanding strategic resource management across three distinct currencies and a deep skill tree. Unlock and upgrade diverse units, from infantry to tanks and aircraft, adapting your approach as you crush enemy bases across varied battlefields. Cannon Clicker offers a surprisingly thoughtful blend of clicker progression and tactical warfare, making it a compelling buy for genre fans.
