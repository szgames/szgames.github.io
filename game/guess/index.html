About Guess

The release date for Guess is July 27, 2026, launching on PC. This calm, elegant word puzzle challenges you to solve words from their definitions, then carry the earned letters forward to the next challenge, creating an endless chain of vocabulary.

Unlike typical word games, Guess ensures you're never truly stuck, offering progressive hints like examples, synonyms, and revealed letters as you try. You can even skip a word if you're truly beaten. Engage in the Endless Mode to build your personal streak, or tackle the shared Daily Challenge, which resets each day. If you enjoy a relaxing vocabulary challenge with a satisfying chain mechanic, this is a definite wishlist for a quiet evening.
