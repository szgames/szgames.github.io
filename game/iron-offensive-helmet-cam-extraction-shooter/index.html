About Iron Offensive: Helmet Cam Extraction Shooter

The release date for Iron Offensive: Helmet Cam Extraction Shooter is July 29, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This title from GamesTrilla Studio promises an intense, fast-paced tactical shooter experienced through a first-person helmet cam, dropping you into immediate combat against advanced AI, artillery, and drones.

Unlike many extraction games, Iron Offensive foregoes inventory loss upon death, opting instead for quick 5-second respawns and a focus on skill-based extraction of geolocated gear and intelligence to earn currency. Manage your licenses for armored vehicles and deploy powerful killstreaks like FPV drones and tanks to dominate the battlefield. If you want an arcade-style, high-intensity tactical extraction shooter without the punishing inventory mechanics, keep this on your wishlist.
