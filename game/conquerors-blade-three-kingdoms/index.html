About Conqueror's Blade: Three Kingdoms

The release date for Conqueror's Blade: Three Kingdoms is July 23, 2026, for PC. Developed by Booming Tech, this title expands the existing tactical framework into the collapse of the Eastern Han Dynasty, mixing third-person hero combat with large-scale unit management.

While the series is known for its siege warfare, this entry focuses heavily on the historical friction of the era, allowing you to recruit elite formations like the Tiger and Leopard Cavalry. Success depends on balancing your hero’s presence on the front lines with the precise execution of unit counters and formations. It is a massive multiplayer strategy game built for those who want to coordinate complex sieges with allies rather than fighting solo. Keep this on your radar if you want a gritty, troop-focused take on Lu Bu’s era.

Strategy enthusiasts should wishlist this for the July launch.
