About World of Eggs: Idle Adventures

The release date for World of Eggs: Idle Adventures was July 24, 2026, on PC. This strategy game attempts to scale the complexity of a global civilization builder into a format that runs entirely on its own, automating the heavy lifting of resource gathering and crafting. Unlike many clicker games that require constant attention, the focus here is on high-level economic planning and a player-driven market where you can rent out items to others.

With over 100 unique units and 200 items to collect, the game is built around long-term progression and cooperative boss raids rather than manual reflexes. The primary hook is seeing how deep a strategy can go when you only have to set the initial direction before the automation takes over. If you want a spreadsheet-style empire that grows while you are offline, this is one to check out.
