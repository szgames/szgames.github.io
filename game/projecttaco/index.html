About Project:Taco

The release date for Project:Taco is July 19, 2026, on PC. This indie collection focuses on fast-paced minigames centered around taco collection and resource management, supporting both solo play and local multiplayer.

The game splits its time between a high-score chasing endless mode and a more structured energy management simulation. While the endless mode offers an extreme variant for high-difficulty taco catching, the power plant mode shifts the pace toward keeping a city powered through reactive strategy tasks. The inclusion of both cooperative and versus modes for local play makes this a lightweight option for group sessions. It is a straightforward pick for players looking for quick, arcade-style challenges with a sense of humor.

Grab this if you want a simple local multiplayer distraction to play with friends.
