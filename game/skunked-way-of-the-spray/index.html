About Skunked: Way of the Spray

The release date for Skunked: Way of the Spray is July 2026 on PC. This indie title mixes martial arts platforming with arcade-style scoring systems as you fight to dismantle the minions of Malodor across twenty distinct levels.

As a ninja skunk, the mechanical hook is the Skunk Shot, a projectile that acts as a crowd control tool to freeze enemies while you maintain momentum. The game is structured around high-speed mobility and chaining combos to maximize high scores or clear time trials. With fifty-four achievements and specific idol discovery runs, it targets players who enjoy technical movement and perfecting stage routes for speedrun leaderboards.

Wishlist this for a sharp, high-intensity platformer that prioritizes combo logic and aggressive movement.
