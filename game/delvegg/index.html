About Delvegg

The release date for Delvegg on PC was July 20, 2026. This desktop idle simulation lives on your taskbar, letting a small army of dwarves, dragons, and vampires mine resources in the background while you work or browse. It functions as a persistent management layer where hatching eggs leads to a complex pet development system involving fusion and refinement.

The depth here lies in the gear and affix system, which mirrors an action-RPG more than a standard clicker. With hundreds of traits and random affixes, the challenge is optimizing specific pets into specialized roles, whether that is a high-damage boss raider or a dedicated gold farmer. It is a low-friction way to manage a complex build without it demanding your full attention. Check this out if you want a passive progression loop with actual mechanical depth. It is a lightweight but rewarding desktop distraction.
