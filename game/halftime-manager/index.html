About HALFTIME Manager

The release date for HALFTIME Manager was August 14, 2026 on PC. This classic football manager focuses on deep tactics and player decisions, presenting every matchday as a compact TV broadcast complete with a 2D pitch, live ticker, and interactive half-time dressing room.

Manage a club across six nations, each with three league tiers and a shared European cup, as you guide your team to glory. The game offers reproducible simulations where your tactical changes and team talks make the difference. For those who prioritize granular control and a strong matchday presentation in their sports simulations, HALFTIME Manager is a strong purchase.
