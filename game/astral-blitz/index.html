About Astral Blitz

The release date for Astral Blitz is August 7, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

Players will build diverse combat styles by customizing their ship with an arsenal of weapons and enhancements, creating everything from precision fighters to engines of pure chaos. Expect challenging runs and deep progression as you fight for freedom. If you seek a skill-based shooter with endless build variety, mark your calendar for Astral Blitz.
