About Mnema: The Fallen Remembered

The release date for Mnema: The Fallen Remembered is July 27, 2026, on PC. As the founder's heir, you manage a city built atop a bottomless pit, recruiting parties of four to descend into a space where laws and witnesses do not exist. The game focuses on the dark reality of a town that thrives on the disappearance of adventurers, forcing you to handle the logistics of a meat-grinder economy.

This is a tactical, turn-based dungeon crawler where positional formation and resource management are the primary hurdles. The standout mechanic is the total lack of accountability for what happens underground; characters can steal from or abandon one another without consequence once they return to the surface. It demands a cynical approach to party composition and survival in a seeded RNG environment where permadeath is the standard. Pick this up if you want a grim RPG that prioritizes cold logistics and social paranoia over heroic fantasy. This is a day-one purchase for fans of unforgiving dungeon management.
