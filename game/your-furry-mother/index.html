About Your Furry Mother

The release date for Your Furry Mother was July 24, 2026, for PC. This casual desktop utility functions as a persistent, always-on-top companion that lives on your screen while you use other applications. It features a fully animated character capable of voice-to-voice interaction, allowing you to speak directly into your microphone to receive vocalized responses from the software.

The app focuses on high levels of customization, including various outfits and the ability to resize or reposition the character anywhere on your workspace. Its primary appeal is the blend of interactive chat and physics-based animations, operating more as a interactive digital mascot than a traditional game. Users looking for a constant, conversational presence on their desktop will find this to be a straightforward, voice-enabled assistant.

Pick this up now if you want a reactive, customizable desktop companion to keep you company while you work.
