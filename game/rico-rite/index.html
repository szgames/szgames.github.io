About Rico Rite

The release date for Rico Rite is September 9, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This breakout-style roguelike flips the script by having a party of bouncing hero balls do the fighting for you. Players draft unique heroes, each with their own attacks and cooldowns, then strategically aim them into descending swarms of bricks. Chaining ricochets builds a crucial combo meter, speeding up your heroes to clear the screen before the enemies push past your paddle.

With 20 distinct hero balls and 11 unique paddles, the depth comes from combining abilities and maintaining high-speed combat to prevent bricks from reaching your line. Anyone looking for a genuinely fresh take on action roguelikes should keep an eye on this one.
