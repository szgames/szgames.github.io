About Shelves and Sorcery: Tidy Up the Enchanted Shop

The release date for Shelves and Sorcery: Tidy Up the Enchanted Shop is August 11, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This cozy fantasy organization simulator challenges players to restore order to a magical shop by meticulously sorting hundreds of enchanted weapons, glowing potions, spellbooks, and curious trinkets. It offers a relaxing experience built around the simple joy of bringing order to chaos, whether tidying a small corner or perfecting every display over hours.

Your once beautiful shop has been left in disarray, requiring you to pick up every item and place it where it belongs, slowly transforming a chaotic mess into a storefront to be proud of. With options for different shop sizes, you can tailor your session to either a quick twenty minutes or an entire evening of unwinding. If you enjoy highly detailed tidying puzzles with a charming fantasy twist, then this is an essential wishlist.
