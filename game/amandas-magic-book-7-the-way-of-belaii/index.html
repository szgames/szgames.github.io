About Amanda's Magic Book 7: The Way of Belaii

The PC release date for Amanda's Magic Book 7: The Way of Belaii is July 20, 2026. This seventh installment in the long-running series sends Amanda and her uncle through the pages of an ancient manuscript to assist a civilization facing imminent destruction. It is a dedicated casual experience that mixes narrative progression with classic puzzle mechanics.

The loop centers on solving Match-3 boards across five story chapters to gather the resources needed to protect the Belaii people. Success relies on managing powerful bonuses and unlocking upgrades to clear increasingly complex boards as the threat to the tribe grows. It is a straightforward, relaxing pick for those who enjoy efficiency-based resource gathering and standard tile-matching without the pressure of high-action genres.

Get this if you want a familiar, well-paced puzzle adventure to round out your collection.
