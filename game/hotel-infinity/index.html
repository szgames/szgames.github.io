About Hotel Infinity

The release date for Hotel Infinity is July 24, 2026, for PC. This simulation-adventure title uses a high-stakes loop mechanic where any failure to spot a deviation in your surroundings resets your entire progress to the start. Success depends on your ability to memorize a standard environment and identify minute changes like shifting shadows or altered wallpaper colors.

The game leans into the uncanny valley of liminal spaces, forcing players to move forward or backward based on visual intuition. It is a focused test of observation that trades traditional combat for a psychological grind through repetitive, evolving corridors. Sharp-eyed fans of memory-based puzzle games should wishlist this to track its progress toward launch.
