About EverRail

The release date for EverRail is July 20, 2026, on PC. In this open-world survival game, you command a massive, mobile train across the frozen moon of Inara, either solo or in co-op for up to four players. Your train acts as a rolling base that must stay in motion to survive, creating a high-stakes management loop where stopping to scavenge for resources drains a finite battery that only recharges while moving.

The standout mechanic is the Permadeath Lite system, where the population of your train serves as your total lives. When your current character dies in the frozen wastes, a different passenger takes over, making the rescue of survivors from abandoned facilities a literal extension of your health bar. You will need to balance expedition length against power consumption while attaching hundreds of wagons to expand your production and logistics. Survival depends entirely on your ability to maintain momentum and upgrade your rolling fortress before the battery hits zero.

Buy this if you want a survival builder that prioritizes logistics and high-pressure resource management over static base defense.
