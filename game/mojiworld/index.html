About Mojiworld

The release date for Mojiworld is August 2026 on PC, delivering a hand-pixeled 2D side-scrolling adventure. This upcoming RPG aims to capture the cozy, chibi-forward feel of early 2000s online worlds, starting as a complete single-player experience with multiple classes to choose from.

Notably, while it launches as a solo adventure, the game is built with a full online MMORPG world planned for post-launch, promising guilds, raids, and shared experiences down the line. Anyone yearning for nostalgic side-scrolling exploration with future multiplayer ambitions should wishlist this for its retro charm.
