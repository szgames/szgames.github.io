About Renz Village Adventure

The release date for Renz Village Adventure was August 13, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC.

This unique idle village simulation RPG blends town-building with automatic hero adventures, letting you construct a bustling settlement and watch your recruited warriors, mages, and healers explore wilderness and battle monsters. Players will upgrade houses, shops, and barracks to strengthen their community, providing meaningful benefits to heroes as they gain experience, collect loot, and defend the growing kingdom. If you enjoy hands-off progression combined with strategic building, this August 2026 release is available now.
