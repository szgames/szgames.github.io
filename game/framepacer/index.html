About framepacer

The release date for framepacer on PC is July 22, 2026. This utility is designed to solve the common issue of erratic frame delivery that makes even high-performing PC hardware feel less smooth than a dedicated console. By forcing a perfectly flat line for frametimes, it addresses micro-stuttering that in-game limiters and driver-level software often miss.

Rather than pushing for the highest raw FPS count possible, this tool focuses on consistency. It allows you to lock your framerate and redirect that saved GPU overhead toward higher resolution or ray tracing settings while keeping your hardware cooler and quieter. The ability to adjust targets mid-game without a restart makes it a practical choice for anyone sensitive to stutter in demanding titles. Use this to stabilize your visuals and maximize graphical fidelity without the jank of fluctuating performance. Buy it if you prioritize fluid motion over raw benchmark numbers.
