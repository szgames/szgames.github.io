About Project F.A.L.S.E.

The release date for Project F.A.L.S.E. is July 22, 2026, on PC. Set in the fallout of a 2042 energy experiment gone wrong, this 2D action game casts you as a military agent tasked by a global coalition to stabilize a world collapsing from environmental decay and high mortality rates.

The gameplay revolves around mission-based levels that require a mix of silent elimination and direct firefights against terrorists, mutants, and zombies. Success depends on managing a wide arsenal ranging from knives and revolvers to improvised tools while navigating hazardous weather and air pollution that can kill just as quickly as the enemy troops. It aims for a specific niche of tactical arcade shooting where resourcefulness in a wasteland matters more than raw power. If you want a mission-driven shooter with a heavy focus on atmospheric survival and hostage rescue, keep this on your radar for 2026.
