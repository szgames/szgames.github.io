About Tomb Raider: Catalyst

The release date for Tomb Raider: Catalyst is December 31, 2027, on PC, PlayStation, and Xbox. This entry moves the series to Northern India, focusing on a mythical cataclysm that has surfaced ancient forces and attracted rival treasure hunters to the region. Built on Unreal Engine 5, it promises the largest world in the franchise's history, emphasizing vertical traversal and high-tech equipment over traditional survival tools.

The shift toward a broader, more open-ended environment suggests a greater focus on non-linear exploration and complex puzzle-solving within the tombs themselves. While earlier entries leaned heavily into Lara's personal growth, this title pivots toward a high-stakes race against international competitors, testing her ability to manage fragile alliances. Action-adventure fans should wishlist this for the promise of a massive, modern archaeological sandbox.
