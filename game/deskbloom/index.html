About DeskBloom

The release date for DeskBloom is August 27, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

This unique simulation integrates directly into your PC desktop, letting plants grow and bloom based on your actual typing, studying, or work rhythm without interfering with your tasks. It supports dragging plants between multiple monitors and even features offline growth, alongside collectible rarities through seeds and hybrid breeding. For those seeking a relaxing, unobtrusive companion for their workspace, DeskBloom is worth a pre-order.
