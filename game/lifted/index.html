About LIFTED

The release date for LIFTED was July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by Adventure Works L.L.C., this title blends platforming and time-travel heists into an adventure inspired by 1980s cinema. You take on the role of Ari, a student drafted into manual labor by his professor to navigate history using a temperamental elevator that bridges time and space.

Success depends on mastering a fluid movement system to solve environmental puzzles while evading tomb raiders and environmental disasters. The game distinguishes itself with a heavy focus on cinematic presentation and voiced characters, avoiding the typical isolation of indie platformers. The central mechanic involving unpredictable landings in different eras ensures that even simple retrieval missions turn into high-stakes navigation challenges. Buy this if you want a polished, character-driven adventure with a sense of humor.
