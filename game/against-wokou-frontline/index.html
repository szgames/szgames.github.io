About Against Wokou Frontline

The release date for Against Wokou Frontline is July 19, 2026, on PC. Developed by YiXia Studio, this tower defense title is set during the historical Wokou pirate raids of the 16th-century Ming Dynasty. You manage coastal defenses against invading rōnin and smugglers by deploying specific soldier types and coordinating tactical formations to hold the shoreline.

The game distinguishes itself by grounding its strategy in a specific period of Jiajing-era history rather than generic fantasy. Success relies on earning stars to unlock specialized units and managing skill points to outpace the increasing numbers of the raiding forces. It focuses heavily on unit synergy, where the right combination of soldiers triggers bonuses that are necessary to survive the later, more aggressive incursions.

History enthusiasts and strategy players looking for a disciplined, period-accurate tower defense challenge should wishlist this one.
