About NEKOHERO

The release date for NEKOHERO is July 21, 2026, for PC. This physics-based action title tasks you with launching a variety of cats out of cardboard boxes to build makeshift bridges across stage hazards.

Success depends on managing the unpredictable weight and movement of 51 distinct feline types as they pile up to form a path toward the heroine. With random character selection and trophies awarded for speed and efficiency, the challenge lies in adapting to the chaotic ways the cats interact with the environment. It is a lighthearted mechanical puzzle that focuses on trajectory control and stacking logic.

Buy this if you want a playful, casual physics puzzler with high replayability.
