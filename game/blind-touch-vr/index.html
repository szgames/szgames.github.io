About Blind Touch VR

The release date for Blind Touch VR was August 11, 2026, and it is available on PC platforms.

This unique VR simulation places you in a world of complete darkness, challenging you to navigate and rebuild your surroundings after losing your sight and memory. Your guide cane becomes your sole 'eye,' allowing you to illuminate objects through touch, while ambient sounds like distant footsteps or subway trains, along with your own memory, help you orient yourself. It's a profound sensory journey that explores perception beyond sight, making it a compelling experience for those seeking deep engagement. You can buy Blind Touch VR for its truly innovative and affecting gameplay.
