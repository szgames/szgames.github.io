About Ghost of Yōtei: Echoes of Sekigahara

The release date for Ghost of Yōtei: Echoes of Sekigahara is October 1, 2026, launching exclusively on PlayStation. From Sucker Punch Productions, this narrative expansion explores Atsu's untold past during the historic Battle of Sekigahara, juxtaposed with new present-day threats in a previously unseen valley in Ezo.

Players will uncover Atsu's key moments before her quest for vengeance, encountering new enemies like Nagato, a figure from her past, alongside new heavy weapons and distinct armor sets. It promises a fresh look at familiar lore, expanding the world with deeper character insight. Ghost of Yōtei: Echoes of Sekigahara looks like essential playing for fans of Sucker Punch's historical action adventures.
