About Yakutori - Japanese screen capture translator

The release date for Yakutori - Japanese screen capture translator is August 30, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

This utility offers a powerful solution for players of untranslated Japanese games and visual novels, providing real-time, offline screen translation. It performs all OCR and English translation locally on your machine, overlaying the results directly onto your game window without any cloud connection, ensuring complete privacy. Beyond just playing, Yakutori also supports language learning with built-in furigana, a Japanese dictionary, and one-click flashcard export.

This is an essential tool for anyone wanting to dive into import Japanese titles. Plan to buy it if you're a dedicated fan of untranslated visual novels or games.
