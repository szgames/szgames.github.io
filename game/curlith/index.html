About Curlith

The release date for Curlith on PC was July 22, 2026. This tactical sports sim blends authentic stone physics with a deckbuilding layer to modernize the traditional rink. You are not just aiming for the button; you are managing a hand of cards that modify your stones with lane shifts and double curls to navigate complex board states.

Success depends on reading a dynamic ice sheet that changes with every delivery. While local matches offer head-to-head competition for two players, the single-player challenge mode provides the real test through objective-based puzzles on procedurally generated ice. It is a precise game of positioning where the cards you draft before the match determine your defensive and offensive options. Sports fans looking for a thoughtful, turn-based take on the sport can play it now after its July launch.
