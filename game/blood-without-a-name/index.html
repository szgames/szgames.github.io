About Blood Without a Name

The release date for Blood Without a Name is July 2026 on PC. This dark fantasy life sim follows the life of an illegitimate noble son across three distinct life stages, beginning in a childhood where survival depends more on listening than speaking. You must manage your reputation and personal stats like Guile and Resolve while navigating the lethal politics of House Veyrath.

The game distinguishes itself through a persistent consequence system where choices made as a child directly impact the alliances and threats available in adulthood. Because you start with no inherent authority, the strategy lies in using your status as an overlooked child to gather secrets and build power before you eventually inherit the family estate. This is a cold, calculated RPG for those who want to play the long game of political maneuvering and family betrayal.

Wishlist this if you want a grounded political simulation where your character's growth is earned through decades of difficult social choices.
