About Cower

The release date for Cower on PC is Coming soon. This pixel idle clicker puts players in charge of a cozy farm, focused entirely on milking one cow to build a vast dairy empire. It distinguishes itself with over 100 upgrades across six tiers and fast 60-second click duels against eight unique bosses, adding a surprising layer of active engagement to the idle genre.

Beyond simple tapping, Cower offers deep progression through stacking combos, unlocking Cowdex variants, researching permanent bonuses, and a Prestige system for endless growth, even with up to 24 hours of offline progress. It's built for those who love seeing dangerously big numbers and want serious clicker depth for short breaks or long sessions. Fans of idle games with active combat should wishlist this one.
