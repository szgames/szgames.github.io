About Sneaky Fishy

The release date for Sneaky Fishy was July 25, 2026, on PC, and the game is now available. This 2D indie puzzle platformer stars a fish that has grown legs to take revenge on the oil rig polluting its habitat. You will navigate the industrial structure by alternating between swimming through water and walking across dry land to disable security measures.

The gameplay centers on a kick mechanic used to smash cameras, redirect oil barrels, and shove boxes onto pressure plates. While the tone is lighthearted, the focus is on environmental destruction as you utilize grapple points and physics-based puzzles to reach the facility owner. It is a focused, objective-driven experience for those who want a tactile platformer with a clear goal. You can now cause mechanical chaos and reclaim the ocean.
