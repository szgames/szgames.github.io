About Don’t Pop!: Splash Race

The release date for Don’t Pop!: Splash Race is July 20, 2026, on PC. This physics-based party racer challenges up to eight players to navigate 3D obstacle courses while controlling a fragile water balloon that bursts upon contact with hazards.

The game relies on bouncy movement and short, high-stakes rounds where one mistake ends your run instantly. While there is a single-player mode for practice, the primary draw is the chaotic online multiplayer and the token-based customization system for your character. It trades complex racing mechanics for unpredictable, silly physics where surviving the course is just as difficult as beating your opponents to the finish line.

Buy this if you want a low-stakes, high-chaos platformer to play with a group of friends.
