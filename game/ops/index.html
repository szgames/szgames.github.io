About Ops

The release date for Ops is July 19, 2026, launching exclusively for PC. Developed by actual Very Large Array operators, this title tasks you with defending rural New Mexico using a massive energy weapon disguised as a radio telescope.

This is a fast-paced arcade shooter where your primary goal is protecting local cattle from extraterrestrial abduction and predatory chupacabras. The challenge lies in thermal management; your antennas will melt under concentrated alien fire, requiring you to prioritize targets and time your shots to prevent equipment failure. It is a focused, high-stakes defense game designed for players who enjoy chasing high scores and managing escalating threats.

Pick this up if you want a quirky, authentic take on classic arcade defense mechanics.
