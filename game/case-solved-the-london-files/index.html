About Case Solved: The London Files

The release date for Case Solved: The London Files was July 29, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This cozy noir mystery challenges players to crack multiple cases in 1960s London, relying solely on pure deduction to unravel murders, conflicting witness statements, and deceptive alibis.

Each investigation presents a closed logic puzzle with a single, provable answer, requiring players to gather suspect information and environmental clues without any guesswork. The hand-drawn 2D scenes and gentle jazz-inspired soundtrack enhance the relaxed pace of this brain teaser. Puzzle enthusiasts who enjoy classic deduction challenges can now play this one.
