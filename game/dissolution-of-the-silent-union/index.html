About Dissolution of the Silent Union

The release date for Dissolution of the Silent Union is July 28, 2026, launching on PC. Set within a crumbling high school on the final day of term, this narrative adventure follows the fracturing relationship between two girls through a series of cyclical, surreal encounters.

This is a bite-sized psychological horror experience designed to be finished in one sitting. It differentiates itself through a striking visual style that layers pixel art and anime character designs over brutalist photography. Rather than traditional jumpscares, the tension relies on the discomfort of liminal spaces and a fragmented script that reveals the truth of Vera’s situation through environmental anomalies. It is a focused, moody piece for those who appreciate experimental storytelling and unsettling atmospheres over complex mechanics. Grab this if you want a short, atmospheric descent into a distorted reality.
