About High Times - Dating/Cooking Sim

The release date for High Times - Dating/Cooking Sim was July 23, 2026, on PC. Developed by Yangyang Mobile, this simulation blends time-management kitchen work with a narrative focused on emotional resolution.

You manage The Hotbox, a cafe where donuts act as mood-altering tools to influence the internal lives of thirty distinct customers. While the culinary side involves discovering recipes and upgrading equipment, the narrative weight lies in the closure simulator, which tasks you with confronting former partners you previously ghosted. It is a specific take on the cozy genre that replaces generic sweetness with the messier realities of past relationship trauma and fully voiced character arcs. The inclusion of high-profile voice talent suggests a heavy emphasis on dialogue and tone over simple high-score chasing.

Grab this if you want a social simulator that prioritizes mending past bridges over building new ones.
