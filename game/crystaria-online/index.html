About Crystaria Online

The release date for Crystaria Online is August 18, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This free-to-play text-based MMORPG aims to capture the depth of classic MMOs with a unique focus on progression up to level 20,000 across multiple realms, from the overworld to floating islands and the Voidheart.

Experience a persistent, living realm that updates under your feet, where 18 classes with distinct resource loops drive powerful builds, and guild wars rage over crafted supplies. Its seasonal Shifting Labyrinth and 81 unique dungeons ensure a constant stream of challenges for players to master. For players seeking a truly deep, old-school MMORPG experience without heavy graphical demands, Crystaria Online demands a wishlist for its ambition.
