About Duelgrounds

The release date for Duelgrounds is August 28, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This 1v1 first-person shooter pushes players to master a constantly shifting arsenal, as each round randomly assigns a new weapon to both combatants.

Beyond pure aim, success hinges on strategic resource management for limited items like grenades and adapting to diverse map layouts. For those seeking a break from competitive duels, the game also offers a six-player Prop Hunt mode, transforming the experience entirely. Consider wishlisting this if you crave a high-skill, adaptable shooter.
