About Blue Guy Must Die

The release date for Blue Guy Must Die is August 19, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

This survivors-like action game casts you as Blue Guy, an elite SWAT member battling waves of toxic bugs with auto-firing weapons. While its Early Access build focuses on the Forest zone, the full release will expand into Desert and Factory areas, promising more challenges. Fans of the genre looking for a new wave-based challenge against a mutated insect threat should mark their calendars.
