About Pixel Q (Your Personal AI Agent)

The release date for Pixel Q (Your Personal AI Agent) is July 18, 2026, on PC. Operating as a local AI assistant, this utility replaces cloud-based subscriptions with a one-time purchase that keeps all your data on your own hardware. It uses five specialized models to manage scheduling, analyze files, and execute voice-commanded tasks without an active internet connection.

While the assistant can control your mouse and keyboard, it functions under a strict permission-based architecture rather than running autonomously. Every high-risk action, from modifying code to shutting down the system, requires manual authorization through a pop-up confirmation. This design caters to users who need the efficiency of a digital butler but refuse to compromise on privacy or system security. It is a specialized tool for anyone looking to automate their workflow through a local, private LLM framework.

Grab this if you want a permanent, subscription-free AI assistant for your desktop.
