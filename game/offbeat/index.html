About OFFBEAT

The release date for OFFBEAT was July 22, 2026, and it launched exclusively for PC. Set in the 1980s, this simulation casts you as a freelance composer starting with a plastic toy keyboard and a basic 4-track recorder. You build a career by scoring everything from corporate videos to fantasy films, slowly replacing your humble gear with high-end synths and oddities like singing garden gnomes found in local classified ads.

The hook here is that the game functions as a legitimate, simplified digital audio workstation. Every instrument is fully simulated, and the tracks you produce for your clients are exported as actual WAV files to your computer. It is a music tycoon game that serves as a genuine entry point for real-world production without the usual software overhead. Buy this if you want a creative sandbox that leaves you with a finished portfolio of actual music.
