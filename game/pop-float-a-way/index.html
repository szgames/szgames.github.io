About POP Float a-way

The release date for POP Float a-way is September 1, 2026, bringing its unique multiplayer party platforming to PC. This chaotic racing game challenges up to eight players to dash through obstacle courses by yelling into their microphones, attempting to knock rivals off track without popping themselves.

The central twist revolves around how loud you scream: go aggressive to boost and collide, but risk becoming more fragile to hits. Proximity chat enhances the mayhem, ensuring you hear every rival's cry as they careen off the map. If you crave a simple, loud, and genuinely unpredictable party experience, make sure to wishlist POP Float a-way.
