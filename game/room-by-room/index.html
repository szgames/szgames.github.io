About ROOM by ROOM

The release date for ROOM by ROOM is currently listed as coming soon for PC. Developed by SASÁ, this 3D puzzle adventure places you in a sequence of isolated chambers where progression relies entirely on scrutinizing the environment for hidden mechanical and visual logic.

The game avoids distractions to focus on pure spatial problem-solving. Success depends on your ability to connect cryptic environmental clues to locked doors and secret codes within standalone scenarios. It is a focused experience for players who want a direct test of observation without a sprawling narrative or complex controls to get in the way. If you enjoy the methodical pace of a traditional escape room, keep this one on your radar.
