About DarkRay

The release date for DarkRay is July 19, 2026, on PC. In this post-apocalyptic adventure, you navigate a ruined world from the perspective of an ordinary cat trapped in a radioactive underground network of bunkers and flooded tunnels. With the surface cut off by landslides and fallout, your survival depends on finding an alternative exit through a multi-level maze of increasingly dangerous mutated territory.

The core experience relies on high-stakes stealth and environmental puzzle-solving rather than direct combat. You must use feline agility to squeeze through tight cracks and manipulate sounds to distract predatory rats and spiders that are significantly larger and faster than you. Success hinges on mastering the verticality of the sewers and knowing when to vanish into the shadows before being spotted. It is a focused, atmospheric take on the survival genre where being small is your only real advantage.

Wishlist this if you want a tense, stealth-heavy descent into a dark world from a unique physical perspective.
