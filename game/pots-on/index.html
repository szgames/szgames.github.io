About Pot's On!

The release date for Pot's On! was July 31, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. In this unique autobattler roguelite, you play as an adventurer defeated by a witch and transformed into a cooking pot. Your goal is to climb a tower, defeat strangely beautiful monster girls, and reclaim your body by efficiently packing their materials into your cramped inventory.

Success hinges on strategically arranging monster materials within your limited pot space to build powerful synergies, which in turn make MSG rain faster to reduce item cooldowns. This core loop elevates item management beyond simple collection, making every inventory slot a critical decision. If you enjoy deep inventory puzzle mechanics wrapped in a quirky premise, check out Pot's On! for a fresh take on the autobattler genre.
