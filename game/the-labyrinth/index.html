About The Labyrinth

The release date for The Labyrinth is July 30, 2026, launching on PC. This first-person co-op horror experience throws up to six players into a living maze that constantly reshapes itself, making memorization useless.

What makes this stand out is the strategic use of your flashlight and voice chat: light reveals creatures but also your position, while noise attracts what hunts you. You'll need to work closely with allies, reviving them quickly and activating altars to unlock the exit, all while the darkness becomes increasingly dangerous. Prepare for tense, teamwork-driven survival where every sound and flicker of light matters.
