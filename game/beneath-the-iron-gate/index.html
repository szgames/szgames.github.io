About Beneath the Iron Gate

The release date for Beneath the Iron Gate was July 22, 2026, on PC. This post-apocalyptic narrative strategy game puts you in control of a cold-chain warehouse turned shelter, where the primary challenge is managing the logistics of human survival while civil order disappears. As Lu Chen, you are the gatekeeper deciding who earns a spot in the Black Box and how to distribute the remaining medicine, fuel, and food among a desperate population.

Success depends on balancing your reputation with concrete evidence against the Ark Society, which controls the city’s dwindling vaccine supply. Unlike typical base builders, the focus here is on the social friction between specialized survivors like tactical officers and radio hosts whose trust must be maintained to withstand a siege. You will have to decide if exposing corruption is worth the risk of a direct assault on your gates. Management fans and narrative enthusiasts should play this for its high-stakes resource trade-offs.
