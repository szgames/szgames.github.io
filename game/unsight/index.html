About UNSIGHT

The release date for UNSIGHT was July 20, 2026, and the game is available now on PC, PlayStation, Xbox, and Nintendo Switch. Developed by C51 Games, this title places you in the role of a deniable covert agent tasked with infiltrating high-security military zones across the Middle East to dismantle a subterranean global threat.

Success depends on a strict operative doctrine of scouting sentry routes and executing stealthy infiltrations before the situation inevitably turns into a high-tempo shootout. While many shooters focus on pure firepower, this experience prioritizes tactical planning and the use of environment to outmaneuver superior numbers. The shift from quiet espionage to open engagement creates a high-stakes loop where one wrong move triggers a global catastrophe. Fans of tactical shooters should check out this summer launch.

Purchase this now for a focused, high-stakes military operation.
