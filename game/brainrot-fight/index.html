About Brainrot Fight

The release date for Brainrot Fight is July 21, 2026, on PC. Developed by Beijing Star Halo Technology Co., Ltd., this strategy title strips away micromanagement in favor of a pure auto-chess loop. You spend your shop phases merging bizarre creatures like Tire Frogs and Capybara Helicopters to upgrade their rarity and stats before sending them into automated combat.

With over 120 unique entities to collect, the game focuses on discovering synergy between absurd skills and items within a limited gold economy. The hook is the unpredictable visual design of the unit roster, which ranges from Strawberry Flamingos to Fried Chicken Toucans. Strategy fans should expect a straightforward drafting experience centered on unit positioning and star-level progression.

Check this one out for a lighthearted take on the auto-battler genre and wishlist it on Steam ahead of the July launch.
