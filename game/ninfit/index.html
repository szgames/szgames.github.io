About NinFit

The release date for NinFit on PC is July 19, 2026. This indie sports title removes the need for physical controllers or VR headsets by using a standard webcam and AI motion tracking to translate your physical movements into digital sword strikes.

The game targets the fitness-at-home market by pairing a cyberpunk aesthetic with active workout modes that track calorie burn in real-time. Instead of traditional button presses, you use hand gestures to slice through targets, making the accuracy of your real-world positioning the primary mechanic. It includes a highlight feature to record your movement streaks, bridging the gap between casual arcade action and a structured home exercise routine.

Buy this if you want a controller-free workout that gamifies cardio through high-score chasing.
