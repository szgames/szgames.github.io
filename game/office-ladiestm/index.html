About Office Ladies™

The release date for Office Ladies™ was July 27, 2026, for PC. This interactive simulation positions you as an HR consultant within a mandatory training program, engaging with six confident women and making choices that affect your story. You'll solve workplace conflicts and navigate office dynamics like a real middle-aged woman.

ORGON®'s "Zeroption™" technology drives meaningful decisions as you undertake a comprehensive "Personalysis™" and unlock different subscriptions. The game even promises an opportunity to find love amidst the HR challenges. Office Ladies™ offers a unique, entirely safe-for-work take on office life, making it a compelling choice for those seeking quirky, choice-driven simulations.
