About Spheroll

The release date for Spheroll is July 27, 2026, on PC. This indie platformer leans heavily into physics-based movement, requiring precise control over a small sphere as it navigates narrow ledges and floating gaps.

Unlike arcade rollers that provide generous aerial correction, this title focuses on weight and momentum. Success depends on calculating the exact speed needed to clear a jump without overshooting the landing. It is a focused, single-player test of patience where balancing on uneven terrain is just as demanding as the platforming itself. Precision is the priority over speed, making every roll a deliberate calculation of physics.

Fans of technical physics games should wishlist this for a pure test of motor skills.
