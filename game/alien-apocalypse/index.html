About Alien Apocalypse

The release date for Alien Apocalypse is August 21, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This open-world sandbox survival game throws you into a world devastated by an alien invasion, where civilization has collapsed and humanity struggles to endure. Your core mission is to adapt, scavenge for resources, craft tools, and fortify shelters against both the elements and the powerful alien lifeforms now ruling the planet.

What sets Alien Apocalypse apart is its focus on turning the invaders' technology against them. Players must collect and study alien tech, learning to reverse-engineer it into hybrid tools and weapons to reclaim what's left. Whether you face the apocalypse alone or with friends in co-op, survival demands preparation and cunning. This is a definitive wishlist for fans seeking a deep, resource-management survival challenge with a unique twist on alien combat.
