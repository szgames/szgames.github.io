About Rust Lords: Appliance Uprising

The release date for Rust Lords: Appliance Uprising is July 22, 2026, on PC. This retro 2D belt-scroller reimagines the post-apocalypse through the lens of sentient, vengeful kitchen appliances fighting back against the ruins left by humanity. Rather than generic brawlers, you control specialized machines like a speed-focused toaster or a vacuum that uses suction to pull enemies into projectile range.

The game leans heavily into the Saturday-morning cartoon aesthetic, supporting couch co-op for up to four players. Each appliance operates on distinct mechanical hooks, such as a kettle that builds a heat meter to unleash steam-bursts or a heavy fridge for crowd control. It is a specific take on the genre that prioritizes character-driven combat mechanics over simple button mashing. This is an easy choice for groups looking for a fresh cooperative brawler with a weird sense of humor.
