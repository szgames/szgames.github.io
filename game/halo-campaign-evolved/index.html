About Halo: Campaign Evolved

The release date for Halo: Campaign Evolved was July 28, 2026, for PC, PlayStation, and Xbox. Developed by Halo Studios, this project marks a significant shift as the series moves to multi-platform availability, bringing the origin of the Master Chief to a broader audience than ever before.

This is a modernized reconstruction of the 2001 classic, focusing on the high-stakes conflict across Alpha Halo. While the core shooting remains faithful, the inclusion of a Premium Edition offering five days of early access starting July 23 was a major push for Day One engagement. The focus on preserving the original Mark V aesthetic through pre-order bonuses indicated a heavy emphasis on nostalgia for the definitive Xbox shooter.

Grab this if you want the definitive version of the Pillar of Autumn’s crash and the first ring's discovery.
