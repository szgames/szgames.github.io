About Ghost of Kyiv

The release date for Ghost of Kyiv is August 24, 2026 on PC. This 2D bullet-hell shooter puts you in the cockpit, defending your homeland against relentless enemy waves as you upgrade five unique stats like Damage and Attack Speed.

While designed for a quick initial playthrough, with five bosses to defeat, victory unlocks an endless mode for extended challenges.
