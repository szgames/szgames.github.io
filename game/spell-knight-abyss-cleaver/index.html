About Spell Knight: Abyss Cleaver

The release date for Spell Knight: Abyss Cleaver was July 27, 2026, on PC. This indie title focuses on high-speed hack-and-slash action, casting you as a magic-wielding warrior diving into a dark dungeon to clear massive waves of enemies with both blades and spells.

The gameplay leans into a fast-paced growth system where power levels spike quickly during a run. Success depends on manual aiming and dash-based evasion rather than passive stat-checking, making it a more active experience than typical survivors-like games. You will need to manage weapon combinations and movement to survive the sheer density of the monster hordes as the difficulty ramps up.

Buy this if you want a straightforward, satisfying combat loop that rewards aggressive positioning and quick upgrades.
