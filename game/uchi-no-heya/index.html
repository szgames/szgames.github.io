About Uchi No Heya!

The release date for Uchi No Heya! is July 19, 2026, for PC. This management simulation puts you in charge of a traditional sumo stable, tasking you with transforming raw recruits into legendary champions. You will oversee every aspect of their lives, from drilling physical stats and preparing chanko nabe to managing the thin margins of a professional sports budget.

Success depends on navigating six annual grand tournaments and climbing the complex banzuke ranking system. The challenge lies in the attrition; you must mitigate injuries and navigate fierce rivalries while keeping the stable financially solvent. It is a niche, data-driven sports manager that trades flashy action for the steady, methodical progression of a lifelong martial arts career. Potential masters of the ring should wishlist this for its detailed focus on Japanese sumo culture.
