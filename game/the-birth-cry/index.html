About The Birth Cry

The release date for The Birth Cry is July 19, 2026, for PC. Set in the 1990s within the freezing mountains of Virelia, the game follows independent investigator Mikhail Markov as he breaks into the long-shuttered Riverside Maternity Hospital to debunk or confirm local urban legends. What begins as a search for answers regarding a state-sealed facility quickly shifts into a desperate attempt to survive a structure designed to keep something trapped within its walls.

This is a short-form survival horror title designed for a single, high-tension session lasting approximately 30 to 60 minutes. Success depends on resource management and environmental awareness rather than direct combat, as players must navigate abandoned wards and avoid paranormal entities while tracking the source of a mysterious cry. The 1990s setting and focused scope suggest a dense, atmospheric investigation for those who prefer their horror bite-sized and mechanical. Horror enthusiasts looking for a quick, focused scare should wishlist this ahead of its summer launch.
