About A Tale of Dirty Whiskers

The release date for A Tale of Dirty Whiskers is July 21, 2026, on PC. This indie adventure frames a classic whodunit through a household of seven feline siblings and their recently deceased matriarch, Princesa. As a detective, you navigate three in-game days of interrogation and discovery to determine which family member is responsible for her suspicious death.

The narrative relies on the tension between the cozy aesthetic of a cat-filled home and the dark reality of a murder investigation. With shifting backgrounds that reflect the passage of time and distinct backstories for every character, the experience focuses on uncovering complicated sibling rivalries and hidden motives. It is a specific blend of visual novel storytelling and domestic drama tailored for those who want a focused, character-driven mystery.

Add this to your list if you want a short, sharp detective story with a unique thematic hook.
