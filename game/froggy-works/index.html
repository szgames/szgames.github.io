About Froggy Works

The release date for Froggy Works is July 30, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This charming strategy and simulation title offers a unique take on farming, allowing you to build and customize an island farm with a crew of industrious frogs.

What sets Froggy Works apart is its versatile playstyle: you can actively manage your crops and resources, or switch on Auto-Pilot and let your frog workforce take over, balancing water, fuel, power, storage, and debt. Farms can even persist across disconnects and devices, inviting friends to join your self-sustaining operation. Players who enjoy deep simulation with an optional idle component will want to wishlist this one.
