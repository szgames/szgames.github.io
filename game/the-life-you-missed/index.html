About The Life You Missed

The release date for The Life You Missed is July 22, 2026, on PC. This narrative adventure follows a successful professional who arrives at a dilapidated property for a quick renovation and sale, only to find the house forcing a confrontation with their own neglected past through the belongings of a stranger.

The gameplay revolves around a mechanical cycle of daytime exploration and nighttime dreaming. You will investigate a workshop, forest, and lake to find notes and photographs, then recreate specific historical moments—like sitting on a swing at a precise time—to trigger visions of the house's history. A voice recorder serves as a tool for tracking your character's psychological shifts as the distinction between the owner's life and your own begins to blur. The inclusion of a mysterious cat and choice-driven dream sequences suggests a heavy focus on atmosphere over traditional puzzle-solving.

Wishlist this if you want a meditative story about regret and identity.
