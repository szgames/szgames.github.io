About The Others Will Join Shortly

The release date for The Others Will Join Shortly was July 23, 2026, on PC. This title takes the simple mechanics of a classic memory game and places them inside a high-stakes corporate horror setting. As a middle manager in a windowless office, you must track shifting cards on your desk while managing a flashlight to keep unseen threats at bay. Success means getting paid, but failures draw a silent monster closer to your workstation.

The central tension lies in the gaslighting nature of the tasks; cards change subtly between rounds, forcing you to constantly doubt your own observations. It is a focused strategy game that relies on psychological pressure and observation rather than traditional combat. This is a solid choice for players who want a short, high-tension experience that turns mundane office work into a fight for survival.

Play this if you want a claustrophobic horror game built on memory and paranoia.
