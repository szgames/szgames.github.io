About Null Root

The release date for Null Root was July 27, 2026, on PC. This action roguelite splits your focus between a central daemon core and a free-moving cursor, requiring you to manage two distinct points of origin for your abilities simultaneously. While your core provides a fixed anchor, your cursor acts as a precision tool for deploying firewalls, drawing detonating lines, and triggering tactical blinks to escape digital swarms.

The game favors high-speed decision-making over long-form strategy, with individual runs lasting anywhere from thirty seconds to ten minutes. With twenty-two active abilities and three specialized tech trees, the challenge lies in mastering the spatial relationship between your two control points to survive screen-filling geometric bosses. Fans of bullet-hell precision and experimental control schemes can now experience it. This is a must-play for players who want a fresh, high-intensity twist on the survivor genre.
