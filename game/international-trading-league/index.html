About International Trading League

The release date for International Trading League is August 10, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This massively multiplayer economic simulation challenges players to build an industrial empire across the globe, producing goods, trading on dynamic markets, and dominating competition in real-world cities.

Unlike simpler simulations, its core appeal lies in chaining over 30 realistic production recipes from raw materials into finished goods, all influenced by live market prices in cities like New York, London, and Tokyo. Aspiring tycoons who want to master complex global supply chains and player-driven economies should wishlist this title.
