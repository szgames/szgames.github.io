About TONK

The release date for TONK on PC is July 23, 2026. This multiplayer tank battler focuses on volume and variety, featuring over 100 unlockable vehicles across three distinct game modes and specific challenge missions designed to unlock fan-favorite units.

Prospective players should note that TONK is an unfinished project being released after its development team disbanded. While it lacks final polish, it provides a functional framework for experimenting with different builds and upgrades throughout its match-based progression. It is a raw, content-heavy arcade experience for those who prioritize building crazy combinations over a refined presentation.

Pick this up if you want a low-stakes vehicle brawler and can overlook the rough edges of an incomplete release.
