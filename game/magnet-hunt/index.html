About Magnet Hunt

The release date for Magnet Hunt is July 28, 2026, launching exclusively for PC. Developed by JA Studio, this arcade-style title focuses on a singular mechanic where you guide a heavy magnet down a hazardous vertical shaft of ocean water to secure treasure on the seabed.

Success depends entirely on managing momentum and descent speed while weaving through moving fish and static obstacles. The game differentiates itself by tying its three difficulty levels to specific physics rules rather than just increasing enemy counts, meaning the weight and handling of your magnet change significantly between modes. It is a focused, high-stakes dive designed for short bursts of play where one collision resets the run. Those who enjoy tight, momentum-based movement and arcade score-chasing should keep this on their radar for July.
