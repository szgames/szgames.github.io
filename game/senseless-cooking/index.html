About Senseless Cooking

The release date for Senseless Cooking is July 29, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This cooperative indie strategy title challenges two players to run a hotdog stand while controlling a blind robot and a deaf robot, where communication is the only path to success.

Asymmetric gameplay demands constant verbal coordination: the blind bot handles physical tasks guided by the deaf bot, who manages orders, cooking timings, and repairs system errors and grill fires. If you enjoy unique co-op premises and chaotic communication challenges, keep this on your wishlist.
