About SESTRY

The release date for SESTRY was July 23, 2026, on PC. This detective horror title places you in a town gripped by the "Mirror Killer," where you must investigate crime scenes using a flashlight mechanic that strictly limits what you can discover. You play an investigator tracking two twin sisters and a suspicious neighbor, balancing a case journal against the physical threats of hunger, cold, and a bleeding sanity meter.

Success depends on gathering hard evidence rather than following hunches. Without the right documents, you cannot break through suspect lies during interrogations, and the game features seven distinct endings based on the proof you uncover. The survival layer adds urgency to your detective work, as timed escapes and resource management determine if you reach the truth or become the next victim. Pick this up if you want a disciplined investigative experience that punishes carelessness with permanent consequences.
