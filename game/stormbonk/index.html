About StormBonk

The release date for StormBonk on PC is July 27, 2026. This fast-paced arcade shooter pits a small spacecraft against a violent meteor storm where survival depends on precise movement and aggressive clearing of space debris. The experience centers on high-speed runs and a climbing difficulty curve that forces quick decision-making under pressure.

While the goal involves collecting numbers to appease your ship's systems, the actual challenge lies in managing limited ammunition and avoiding dense clusters of incoming hazards. This is an indie title focused on short, high-intensity sessions where a single mistake ends the run. It targets players who want a pure test of reflexes without complex meta-progression slowing down the action. Buy this if you want a lean, demanding score-chaser for your PC library.
