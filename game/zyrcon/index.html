About Zyrcon

The release date for Zyrcon was July 24, 2026, on PC. Developed by DIGITALICA, this arcade shooter simplifies the bullet-heaven formula into a mouse-only defense game where your ship tracks the cursor and fires automatically. Success depends on manual resource collection, as you must actively hold a button to pull plasma cores from shattered asteroids before they drift off-screen.

The risk here is a difficulty curve tied directly to your performance rather than a timer; the faster you clear waves and boost your score, the more aggressive the UFO strafing runs and homing asteroids become. With upgradeable weapons like the Ricochet Stasis and a time-freeze mechanic to manage crowded screens, it targets players who want high-speed sessions that require zero keyboard input. Grab this if you want a pure, one-handed leaderboard chase.
