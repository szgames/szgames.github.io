About One More Spin

The release date for One More Spin is July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by EXP Interactive, this casual title blends slot machine aesthetics with incremental growth and risk management.

Instead of traditional gambling, you treat a grid of slots as a resource engine. You place specialized machines like Bomb Slots to trigger chain reactions or MegaSlots for high-energy payouts, stacking multipliers to clear heat levels before time expires. The loop relies on choosing artifacts that synergize with specific fruit symbols or cursor movements to optimize your board. Every run ends with a high-stakes choice: cash out your current earnings or go all-in to hit a target that multiplies your entire pool. Failure means losing it all, making the late-game tension a direct test of your greed.

A smart pick for players who enjoy chasing high-score multipliers and permanent progression upgrades.
