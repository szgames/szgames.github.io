About Magic Mess: Restore Order to the Wizard's Tower!

The release date for Magic Mess: Restore Order to the Wizard's Tower! was August 24, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. This cozy organization simulator tasks players with the immense job of returning 3,040 scattered books, potions, scrolls, and other magical artifacts to their proper places within a chaotic wizard's tower.

As you meticulously tidy each section, you'll unlock new magical abilities to streamline the process, while your loyal dragon companion offers playful distractions. The sheer volume of items promises a substantial and relaxing tidying challenge for those who enjoy methodical, low-stress simulation. You can now experience a satisfying digital decluttering.
