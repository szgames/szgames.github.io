About My Lil Afterlife

The release date for My Lil Afterlife was July 28, 2026, and it is now available on PC.

As the new ruler of the afterlife, you'll summon a colorful array of spirits, from old wise skeletons to sassy sasquatches, to reside in your realm. Personalize your incorporeal form with diverse clothing and accessories, and then decorate your ghostly island however you choose, whether you prefer a spooky corn maze or a tranquil park setting. The game isn't just about decor and friendship; you'll also unravel mysteries by taking on the reapers' tower, using spine-tingling spells to smite your enemies. This creative blend of cozy simulation and light combat offers a fresh, irreverent take on the afterlife. The game is available now if you're ready to reshape the underworld.
