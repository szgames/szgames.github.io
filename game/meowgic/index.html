About Meowgic

The release date for Meowgic is July 20, 2026, on PC. This action-adventure title casts players as feline sorcerers who must chant, shout, or meow into their microphones to trigger various magical effects. While it features a lighthearted fairy-tale aesthetic, the core loop is built around a high-stakes extraction mechanic where you must defend your loot and reach a hot-air balloon to escape safely.

The central hook is a voice-recognition system that allows you to map specific sounds or phrases to your spellbook. This creates a high potential for chaotic accidental damage, as fireballs and levitation spells can affect your teammates just as easily as the monsters. Success depends on coordinating these vocal triggers while navigating floating islands and dungeons with up to three friends. It is a creative choice for groups who want a tactile, noisy alternative to traditional hotbar combat. Buy this if you want a cooperative extraction game that rewards vocal coordination and slapstick experimentation.
