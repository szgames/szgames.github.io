About WE ARE SO DEAD

The release date for WE ARE SO DEAD is August 17, 2026, on PC. This co-op seance horror game puts 2-6 players in a haunted house, but one of you starts dead. As the ghost, you alone see the demon, dangers, and the way out across nine floors, yet your only voice is a Ouija board that your living friends will inevitably misread.

Success hinges entirely on the dead player's ability to communicate complex information under pressure through limited means, while the living try to interpret erratic Ouija movements. Each session introduces a new demon with hidden rules, forcing constant adaptation. Wishlist this if you love asymmetrical co-op with a strong emphasis on deduction and social deception.
