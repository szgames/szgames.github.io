About Masters of Magic

The release date for Masters of Magic is August 25, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This fantasy MOBA redefines spellcasting by letting you chant spells aloud with your own voice, adding a unique layer of immersion and strategy to 4v4 online PvP combat.

Players choose from 20 available spells, each with a shorter basic chant or a longer, more powerful version that carries higher risk. Equipping multiple spells of the same attribute grants affinity boosts, enhancing damage and providing extra buffs. With Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, and Bear the Crown modes already in early access, this game offers a fresh take on competitive magic. If the voice recognition holds up, this is a definite wishlist for MOBA fans seeking innovation.
