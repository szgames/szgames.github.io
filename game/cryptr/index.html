About Cryptr

The release date for Cryptr is July 18, 2026, arriving exclusively for PC. This 1-4 player co-op hack-and-slash focuses on party-based dungeon crawling, where players can team up with friends or utilize AI allies to clear corrupted zones across four distinct regions. By combining traditional class archetypes with an ascension system, you can evolve basic roles like the Knight or Wizard into powerful Paladins and Necromancers.

What sets this title apart is the flexibility of its talent trees and the inclusion of a dungeon-building suite. Rather than relying on purely random generation, the game features hand-built levels with varied objectives, such as relic escorts and spawner hunts, and encourages players to design and share their own custom challenges. The ability to respec for a small gold fee ensures that your build remains adaptable as you reach the difficult boss encounters at the bottom of each crypt. Buy this for a focused, class-heavy dungeon crawler with genuine build variety.
