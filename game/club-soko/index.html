About Club Soko

The release date for Club Soko on PC was July 23, 2026. This indie puzzle title puts you in the role of Maude, a graffiti artist navigating an underground nightclub to tag walls while avoiding bouncers and navigating crowds of patrons.

Built around 100 handcrafted levels, the game uses classic sokoban-style logic across six distinct chapters, each featuring its own musical theme. The presence of eleven different puzzle piece types and thirteen cameo characters from across the indie scene suggests a depth that goes beyond a simple aesthetic exercise. It is a focused mechanical challenge that swaps traditional warehouse crates for club culture obstacles like drunk guests and circle pits. Fans of spatial reasoning and street art should check this out now following its July launch.
