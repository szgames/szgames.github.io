About Firewheels Shop Simulator

The release date for Firewheels Shop Simulator is August 28, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This simulation game lets you construct and manage your own diecast model store, focusing on shelf organization, customer service, and shop expansion.

Beyond the typical management loop, the game promises a compelling chase for rare and elusive diecast cars, along with a trading system to engage customers and expand your collection. Players can customize every detail of their shop and secure licenses from top brands to diversify stock. If you enjoy niche simulation games with a collector's focus and co-op potential, prepare to build your diecast empire.
