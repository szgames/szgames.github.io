About Zankoi's Chronicles

The release date for Zankoi's Chronicles was August 14, 2026, on PC, promising a fresh take on roguelike action. This unique title fuses intense real-time 1v1 combat with deep deckbuilding, where every attack, defense, and dodge is a card.

Filling your Poise bar freezes time, allowing you to unleash devastating, custom-built card combos against your foes. With no meta upgrades to lean on, mastery of synergies and combat execution defines each run, as you fight to awaken the Zankoi and reclaim freedom from the Archmage. Fans seeking a truly skill-driven roguelike with strategic depth should certainly play Zankoi's Chronicles.
