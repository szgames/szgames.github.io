About Codename CURE II

The release date for Codename CURE II was July 24, 2026, on PC. Building on the foundation of its free-to-play predecessor, this sequel shifts focus toward tactical squad-based objective missions where five players must coordinate to survive the aftermath of a total biological collapse.

While many zombie shooters lean into chaotic arcade action, this title emphasizes organized military precision. You operate as an elite unit tasked with high-stakes deployment into infected zones, either with a full squad of friends or offline alongside bots. The dynamic mission structure requires constant movement and area control rather than just holding a single chokepoint. The shift to more modern visuals and refined first-person mechanics suggests a much heavier emphasis on weapon feel and squad synergy than the original mod-based entry.

Veterans of the first game and tactical shooter fans can now experience this for high-pressure cooperative play.
