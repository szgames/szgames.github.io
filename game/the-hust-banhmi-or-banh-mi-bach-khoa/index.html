About The Hust Banhmi | Bánh Mì Bách Khoa

The release date for The Hust Banhmi is July 18, 2026, on PC. Operating as a direct sequel to Brother Hai's Pho Restaurant, this simulation shifts the focus from soup to street sandwiches at the iconic Parabol Gate of the Hanoi University of Science and Technology.

While a high-stakes police manhunt for fugitives provides a background narrative for the series, your actual task is the daily hustle of a student entrepreneur. You will manage a food cart alongside your partner, Mr. Gold, preparing ingredients and serving crowds in a technical university setting. It is a niche simulation that trades high-action drama for the specific rhythmic pressure of maintaining a small-scale business in a busy urban environment. Pick this up if you want a casual, culturally specific management loop focused on the grind of Hanoi street food.
