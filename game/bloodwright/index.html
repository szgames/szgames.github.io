About Bloodwright

The release date for Bloodwright was July 28, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This dark fantasy action RPG rogue-lite plunges players into procedurally generated dungeons where every run is a high-stakes gamble. Players must constantly weigh the risk of pushing deeper for greater rewards against losing all their gear to permadeath, with only permanent upgrades and stash items surviving a fall.

Success means claiming powerful loot and discovering unique combinations of weapons, armor, spells, and affixes that can transform your playstyle with devastating synergies. Even failed expeditions contribute to progress, allowing you to unlock permanent upgrades through Sevrik's Soul Tree. If you crave a roguelite where every decision matters and character building is key, Bloodwright is worth playing.
