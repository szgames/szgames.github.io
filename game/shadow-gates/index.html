About Shadow Gates

The release date for Shadow Gates is July 19, 2026, for PC. This indie action-adventure title functions as a roguelite dungeon crawler where failure is the primary resource for progression. You navigate five distinct biomes, including the Frozen Cathedral and Volcanic Forge, attempting to clear gates that scale in difficulty through five levels of ascension.

The central hook is a shadow extraction system that allows you to resurrect defeated enemies to fight alongside you in subsequent attempts. With five classes ranging from the blink-dodging Rogue to the area-cleaving Knight, the challenge lies in balancing your active combat skills with a growing army of up to five shadows. This is a targeted pick for those who want a power fantasy built on a cycle of persistent, incremental growth through defeat.

Grab this if you want a dark, tactical crawler that rewards your worst runs with your strongest reinforcements.
