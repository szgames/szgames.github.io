About Humans Are Cats -- Catification Action

The release date for Humans Are Cats -- Catification Action is August 2026 on PC. This indie racing game casts you as MOUSE, an alien investigator sent to Earth to uncover why a city's residents are turning into cats. You'll run, jump, slide, and flash-dash through neon streets, using your Meow Wave to scan suspicious targets while avoiding enemies.

The core of the game is an endless high-score chase where you balance risk and reward to maintain combos and score multipliers. Graze hazards for bonus points or play it safe, pushing deeper into the cat-infested city until your HP runs out. Fans of skill-based endless runners should keep this on their radar.
