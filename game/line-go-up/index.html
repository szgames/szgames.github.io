About Line Go Up

The release date for Line Go Up is July 21, 2026, and it is launching exclusively on PC. This corporate-themed roguelike deckbuilder casts you as a CEO tasked with satisfying shareholders by acquiring competitors and managing a volatile market through deck-based strategy.

Success depends on balancing aggressive expansion with the risk of firing too many staff or failing to navigate global calamities. With over a hundred companies to acquire and a thousand project combinations, the core challenge lies in a high-stakes gambling loop where a single bad investment can end your career or the world. You must constantly weigh the benefits of hiring new executives against the threat of unexpected market incidents that force difficult trade-offs.

Buy this if you want a cynical, fast-paced strategy title that treats global economics like a dangerous game of chance.
