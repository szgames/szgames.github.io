About Lou's Lagoon

The release date for Lou's Lagoon was August 26, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This cozy adventure simulation tasks you with flying a seaplane across a tropical archipelago, collecting resources, and rebuilding communities after a storm, all while searching for your missing Uncle Lou.

Its core loop combines exploration and crafting, using a versatile Swirler tool to gather materials from wildlife and plants, essential for repairing buildings and crafting goods. Expect charming islanders and distinct biomes like Tangle Bloom, Gleam Reef, and Boulderia, each with their own unique problems. Fans of chill, narrative-driven crafting games with exploration can now enjoy this one.
