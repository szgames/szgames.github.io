About Arctic Shift: Fuel Station

The release date for Arctic Shift: Fuel Station was July 24, 2026, on PC. Developed by Wildbyte, this simulation title places you in charge of a vital refueling outpost buried deep within a harsh, frozen wilderness where business management and survival mechanics collide.

You are responsible for more than just pumping gas and stocking shelves; the core challenge lies in maintaining critical infrastructure against plummeting temperatures and sudden power failures. The game forces a constant trade-off between the safety of your station and the necessity of exploring the surrounding tundra for resources and infrastructure repairs. While travelers provide your steady income, the true pressure comes from a logistics loop where every blizzard can sever your supply lines and leave you stranded. Success depends on upgrading your outpost to handle increasingly brutal weather while uncovering the environmental history of this isolated region.

Anyone looking for a lonely, high-stakes management sim can now play it.
