About Tower Lab

The release date for Tower Lab was September 7, 2026, when it launched on PC. This roguelite tower defense title uniquely discards enemy health bars, instead challenging players to weaponize physics and environmental pushes to clear the map.

Success hinges on drafting a deck of towers, chaining them into intricate contraptions, and manipulating projectile size to fling robot hordes off the edge. With five game modes including a deckbuilding campaign and a level editor, players can experiment endlessly with physics-driven synergies. Play it now if you're ready for a fresh, physics-heavy take on tower defense.
