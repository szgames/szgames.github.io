About Cogfall

The release date for Cogfall is July 24, 2026, on PC. Developed by Nodding Panda Studios, this steampunk dungeon crawler focuses on methodical progression through a subterranean clockwork kingdom where every descent requires a precisely tuned loadout. You manage a resource called Wind to survive mechanical encounters, using a mix of six distinct gear sets and permanent talent upgrades to push deeper into the floors.

The strategic hook lies in the synergy between your gear infusions and your companion's passive abilities. Unlike more frantic action titles, success here depends on your ability to stack Clockwork Blessings after boss fights to solve specific build weaknesses before the difficulty spikes. The inclusion of a talent workshop ensures that even failed runs provide the currency needed to increase your health pool or loot quality for the next attempt. Purchase this if you want a gear-heavy RPG that rewards slow, intentional character builds.
