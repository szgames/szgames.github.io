About Astro Raiders

The release date for Astro Raiders is July 23, 2026, on PC. Developed by Far Side Games, this arcade shooter updates the classic ball-splitting formula with modern power-ups and a cable launcher mechanic across hundreds of levels.

Success relies on precise movement and stage management as you split bouncing orbs into smaller, faster targets. While the core loop focuses on clearing screens, the addition of volcanic hazards and frozen terrain forces you to adapt your positioning to the environment. The variety of character unlocks and global leaderboards provide a clear incentive for those aiming to master the three-star rating on every stage. Pick this up if you want a polished, high-speed arcade experience that prioritizes reaction time over complex controls.
