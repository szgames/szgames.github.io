About Healer

The release date for Healer was August 28, 2026, and it is now available on PC. This action roguelike puts a unique spin on the boss rush format by making you, the healer, solely responsible for keeping a team of unhelpful AI allies alive against the demon lord's lieutenants.

Your "stupid teammates" offer little more than complaints, forcing you to master dodging, precise healing, and survival across 15 bosses and 6 classes, with progression tied to an ability tree featuring custom nodes. This is for players who want a truly challenging and unconventional take on the support role; it is now available if you crave high-skill, low-patience gameplay.
