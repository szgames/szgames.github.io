About Sill Sticks

The release date for Sill Sticks is July 19, 2026, on PC. This simulation functions as an idle desktop companion where a stick figure named Bota lives on the edges of your active windows, muttering to himself and reacting to the actual time of day and day of the week. Unlike more demanding virtual pets, this app is built to remain in the background without obstructing your workflow or requiring constant management.

The appeal lies in the low-stakes observation of Bota's slacker lifestyle, which includes playing games, sleeping, and occasional direct interactions with you. It is a digital mascot for those who want a small sense of company on their monitor while they work or browse. The variety of contextual dialogue ensures his presence feels dynamic rather than repetitive over long sessions.

Pick this up if you want a low-maintenance desktop buddy to watch during your downtime.
