About Incremental Vampire

The release date for Incremental Vampire is July 22, 2026, on PC. This strategy title fuses the survivor-style horde shooter with an incremental progression system built around a massive permanent skill tree. While the screen frequently fills with enemies that require precise dodging and positioning to survive, the game distinguishes itself by letting you keep your rare weapon inventory through prestige resets, removing the sting of starting over.

The challenge centers on boss fights at the end of every run that test your build's damage output against your actual movement skills. Success depends on balancing gem collection with risky upgrades that eventually turn your character into an unstoppable force. It is a focused pick for players who want the power fantasy of a survivor game without losing their best gear when it is time to prestige. Wishlist this if you want a more forgiving take on the genre's reset mechanics.
