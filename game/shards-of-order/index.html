About Shards of Order

The release date for Shards of Order on PC was July 28, 2026. This dark fantasy RPG abandons traditional turn-based combat and mana points in favor of a real-time countdown system where every card played advances the enemy's actions. You lead a trio of survivors who share a single deck, forcing you to balance drawing odds against individual hero specializations as you attempt to repair the shattered laws of reality.

Success depends on managing time as a finite resource during encounters while navigating a world where the dead do not stay buried. Outside of the deckbuilding, the game functions as a full RPG featuring skill trees, equipment customization, and dialogue choices that determine the fate of a fractured world. Fans of tactical card games looking for a high-stakes mechanical twist should check this out now. Buy this if you want a complex, shared-deck strategy experience with heavy consequences.
