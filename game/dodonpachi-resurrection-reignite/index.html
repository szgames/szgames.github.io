About DoDonPachi Resurrection Reignite

The release date for DoDonPachi Resurrection Reignite was August 26, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. CAVE's legendary bullet hell series returns, bringing its signature overwhelming bullet patterns and intense battles of wits to a new generation of players.
