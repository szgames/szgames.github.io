About Star Haul Tycoon

The release date for Star Haul Tycoon is August 6, 2026, on PC. This narrative logistics simulator tasks players with connecting planets and optimizing complex supply chains to pay off a massive corporate debt, all under the watchful eye of Supervisor.exe.

Its dark, corporate sci-fi humor sets it apart, blending deep industrial automation with strategic transport management across procedurally generated worlds and 30 unique resources. Efficiency is paramount, as inefficient routes spell bankruptcy. Fans of complex economy simulations and a bleak comedic tone should absolutely wishlist this title.
