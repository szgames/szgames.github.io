About Under His Eyes

The release date for Under His Eyes was July 23, 2026, and it is available now exclusively for PC. This management strategy title puts you in the position of an ascending crime lord, balancing the logistics of money laundering through legitimate fronts with the violence of territorial expansion. You will have to manage a crew of enforcers whose loyalty is tied directly to their pay, or risk internal betrayal while fighting off rival families.

The game relies heavily on a branching dialogue system where verbal negotiations are as permanent as gunfights. Success depends on your ability to extract protection money and secure resources without triggering a premature gang war through poor diplomatic choices. It is a dense simulation of organized crime that prioritizes decision-making and resource allocation over direct action. Grab this if you want a cold, calculated look at empire building where words carry as much weight as weapons.
