About Deadlock Station

The release date for Deadlock Station was August 19, 2026, and the game is now available exclusively on PC. This tactical rogue-like transforms the battlefield into a weapon, challenging players to win automated battles through precise pre-battle positioning and clever environmental manipulation, from constructing spike walls to luring alien invaders into traps.

Assemble a unique squad with diverse abilities, enhance them with powerful implants, and learn to outwit deadly Ravagers and Renegades. Base development unlocks new items and factions, promising limitless experimentation and deep synergies so no two expeditions ever feel identical. Play it if you seek a fresh take on indirect strategy and squad customization.
