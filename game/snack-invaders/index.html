About Snack Invaders

The release date for Snack Invaders was August 25, 2026, and the game is available now exclusively on PC. This action title casts you as an astronaut who awakens from a junk food coma to find themselves stranded in a galaxy overrun by living junk food, fighting for survival solo or with up to three friends in online co-op.

Don't let the whimsical premise fool you; this action game features over a dozen multi-phase Souls-like bosses with unique attack patterns across more than ten food-themed levels. Players will navigate a points system to purchase gear and upgrades, making every boss defeat crucial for progression. Fans of challenging co-op action with a side of absurd humor should check it out now.
