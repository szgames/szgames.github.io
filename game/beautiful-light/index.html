About Beautiful Light

The release date for Beautiful Light is scheduled for December 2026 on PC. This tactical extraction shooter pits six squads of three against one another in a race to secure and extract valuable artifacts from a high-threat zone. While most players participate as human operators equipped with gadgets and firearms, others can invade these sessions as player-controlled anomalies tasked with hunting down the human teams.

The horror elements and steep learning curve distinguish this title from more arcade-focused shooters. Success depends on careful resource management and squad coordination, as any death results in the loss of your brought gear. By adding the ability for players to control the monsters, it creates an unpredictable threat that AI enemies cannot replicate. Tactical shooter fans looking for a high-stakes horror experience should wishlist this for its late 2026 launch.
