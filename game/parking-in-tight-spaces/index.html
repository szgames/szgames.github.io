About Parking in Tight Spaces

The release date for Parking in Tight Spaces is August 14, 2026, when it arrives on PC. This simulation offers a refreshing take on the parking genre, where precision meets player freedom; instead of rigid rules, the game encourages you to break barriers and create your own path to success.

Featuring over 70 unique vehicles, from cars and buses to tractors and even tanks, each of the 60+ handcrafted levels presents a spatial puzzle designed to be solved however you see fit. While it’s a no-pressure experience, global leaderboards also await those who wish to shave seconds off their runs. This is a definite wishlist for puzzle and simulation players looking for a laid-back challenge with a surprising amount of destructive latitude.
