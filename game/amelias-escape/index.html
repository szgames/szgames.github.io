About Amelia's Escape

The release date for Amelia's Escape is July 23, 2026, on PC with VR support. Playing as a teenager named Amelia, you must solve your way out of a high-stakes escape room after being kidnapped by a scientist obsessed with temporal mechanics. This narrative-driven experience focuses on the friction between captive and captor while you scavenge the environment for tools and clues.

The game distinguishes itself by offering a non-linear approach to its puzzles, allowing you to tackle objectives in the order that makes the most sense to your logic rather than following a rigid script. It accommodates various comfort levels by supporting both teleportation and standard analog movement. VR puzzle fans looking for a character-focused story should keep this on their radar.

Wishlist this if you want a logic-heavy escape room with a specific focus on its two-person cast.
