About Forgotten Haven: The First Call

The release date for Forgotten Haven: The First Call was August 27, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC.

This first-person fantasy puzzle adventure centers on restoring 51 scattered forms by finding, rotating, and placing their pieces within a forgotten haven. Notably, it features no combat or time limits, emphasizing a peaceful, self-paced experience where every completed restoration becomes a permanent part of the world. Players looking for a genuinely tranquil reconstruction challenge can now play this game.
