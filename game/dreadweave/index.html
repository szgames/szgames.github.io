About Dreadweave

The release date for Dreadweave is July 22, 2026, for PC. Developed by DeadGrass Games, this extraction shooter drops you into a frozen Soviet-era town where a secret experiment has fractured reality into two distinct dimensions.

The primary threat is a creature that tracks your heartbeat, making panic a mechanical risk rather than just a player reaction. You must scavenge for resources and rare loot in the physical world while venturing into the oxygen-limited Other Side to find the most valuable gear. Success depends on upgrading your underground bunker and managing your pulse during raids where a single mistake results in the loss of all carried equipment. High-stakes resource management and the constant pressure of the heartbeat mechanic turn every extraction into a tense psychological battle.

Pick this up if you want a punishing extraction loop with a unique stealth twist. Buy it on launch if you enjoy the tension of loss-on-death mechanics.
