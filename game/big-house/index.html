About Big House

The release date for Big House is July 23, 2026, on PC. Set in a future where civilization has succumbed to terminal boredom, you take on the role of a legendary gambler tasked with saving a failing casino through high-stakes psychological warfare.

This strategy title centers on blind betting mechanics where neither side sees the other's hand, forcing you to rely on gut instinct and a pool of 13 random action cards. With 20 distinct opponents and permadeath stakes, the challenge lies in managing a limited resource pool of five crystals and thirty chips while predicting opponent passives. Success depends on outbidding the dealer by as little as a single chip to seize the initiative.

Fans of minimalist roguelike card games should prepare to gamble when the casino doors open in 2026.
