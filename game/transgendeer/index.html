About TransGenDeer

The release date for TransGenDeer is August 28, 2026, coming exclusively to PC. This one-of-a-kind educational jump and run game is designed to fight transphobia and provide facts about transitioning and the LGBTQIA+ community, all presented with lovingly hand-drawn characters and animations, entirely free of AI generation.

As you guide the TransGenDeer to transition, you'll collect 'transition points' by hugging ally bears and gather 'fact coins' to answer quizmaster octopus questions. Develop your character through each level before facing the final boss, with the goal of fostering understanding and allyship. TransGenDeer is a purposeful platformer worth wishlisting for those seeking a game with a strong social message.
