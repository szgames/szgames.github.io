About AI and You

The release date for AI and You was July 23, 2026, on PC. Developed by a veteran engineer, this simulation focuses on the tension between efficiency and oversight as you manage Aya, an artificial assistant that handles your daily labor. While she can free up hours for you to build a career or a studio, she is prone to confident hallucinations that you must verify or ignore at your own risk.

The title is notable for its development process, having been created entirely through AI direction without the developer writing code or drawing assets directly. This meta-commentary on automation translates into a loop of task delegation and prompt-style management where your success depends on how well you brief your software. It is a focused experiment in the cost of outsourcing human thought to a black box.

Pick this up if you want a cynical, grounded look at the future of digital work.
