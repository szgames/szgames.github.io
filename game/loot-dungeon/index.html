About Loot Dungeon

The release date for Loot Dungeon is August 18, 2026, launching on PC. This dark fantasy incremental roguelite ARPG flips the genre on its head, making every death a crucial step in your progression.

Instead of restarting, you literally need to die to unlock permanent upgrades, new characters, and deeper dungeon floors. It distills dungeon crawling into intense, short runs focused on build crafting, where choices on perks and equipment are permanent. This is a buy for roguelite fans seeking a fresh take on meta-progression and high replayability.
