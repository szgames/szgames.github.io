About Space Station Designer

The release date for Space Station Designer is November 17, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

As CEO of a private aerospace company, you'll start with a single module and expand into a network of self-sustaining orbital outposts, ultimately aiming to construct a Lunar Space Elevator.
