About Harvest Dice Profit

The PC release date for Harvest Dice Profit was July 23, 2026. This idle simulation tasks you with cultivating "diceshrooms," peculiar fungi that range from D4s to D20s and serve as your primary source of income. Once harvested, these dice are rolled to generate the capital needed to maintain a growing agricultural operation.

While the initial loop involves manual watering and harvesting, the real focus is on the scale of automation and the looming threat of debt. Unlike many low-stakes casual games, the Hard and Hell modes introduce a fail state where failing to meet scheduled payments ends your run. With 130 upgrades and multiple automated rolling methods, the challenge lies in balancing your infrastructure spending against the compounding pressure of your creditors. Buy this if you want an incremental game that forces you to optimize your economy under a genuine threat of bankruptcy.
