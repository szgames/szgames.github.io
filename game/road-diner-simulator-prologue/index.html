About Road Diner Simulator: Prologue

The release date for Road Diner Simulator: Prologue was July 27, 2026, on PC, and it is available now. Serving as a narrative precursor to the full game, this simulation tasks you with managing the original, un-renovated establishment on Route 66 during its final days of decline. You are responsible for every layer of the workflow, from seating families and traveling salesmen to handling the short-order cooking after the previous chef abandoned the post.

Success relies on high-pressure multitasking rather than just aesthetic management. You must balance the physical demands of wiping tables and washing dishes against the timing requirements of the grill and stove. The challenge lies in the tight staffing situation; managing a surge of patrons alone requires precise prioritization to avoid long wait times and messy floors. It is a focused look at the logistical friction of a failing business before the eventual rebuild.

Grab this if you want a stressful, hands-on management slice before the main game arrives.
