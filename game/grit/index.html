About GRIT

The release date for GRIT was August 18, 2026, launching on PC. This Wild West battle royale adds a unique twist: players build poker hands from found weapons to gain special perks and influence their playstyle.

This system encourages strategic loadout choices, from aggressive gunslinging to prioritizing health regeneration, as you explore towns and plains. For those seeking a fresh take on the battle royale genre, GRIT is worth a close look and is available now.
