About Dinoblade

The release date for Dinoblade was July 23, 2026, on PC, and the game is now available. Developed by Team Spino LLC, this action RPG casts you as a young Spinosaurus armed with a massive Great Sword in a prehistoric world altered by a mysterious cataclysm.

The gameplay focuses on high-stakes melee encounters against rival dinosaurs who have also mastered weapon-based combat. Progress is gated by Alphas, apex predators that function as region bosses, requiring precise parries and dodges to overcome. Success relies on chaining combos and mastering new skills to survive increasingly hostile biomes. After defeating the final boss, players can access a Boss Rush mode to test their combat efficiency against the game's toughest predators.

Fans of technical melee combat and unique creature designs should check out this title for its unusual blend of dinosaur evolution and heavy weapon mechanics.
