About Death Paradise

The release date for Death Paradise was August 29, 2026 on PC. This action-adventure game throws players into the immediate aftermath of a zombie outbreak on Miami Beach, tasking them with surviving the first 24 hours.

You must quickly rescue and customize AI teammates, restore the island's emergency power, and activate an SOS signal before the evolving infected overwhelm everything. The urgency of the 24-hour clock, with more aggressive infected appearing at night, makes finding a generator and getting a signal online a race against time. Fans of zombie survival with team management will want to check this out.
