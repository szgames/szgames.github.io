About The Alley

The release date for The Alley was July 23, 2026, and it launched exclusively for PC. Developed by AIXLAB, this first-person psychological horror title traps you in an endless, maze-like urban corridor where survival depends on your ability to distinguish between real threats and harmless environmental tricks. Using a camera and a glowing bracelet inherited from a Mudang shaman, you must document supernatural occurrences while avoiding a vengeful spirit that hunts you through the narrow passages.

This is a game of observation and identification rather than direct combat. By photographing true aberrations and ignoring deceptive objects, you earn keys needed to unlock the narrative and escape the loop. Success relies on your ability to keep your composure while the ghost's pursuit grows more aggressive with every discovery. Horror fans who enjoy mechanical deduction and atmospheric tension can pick this up now following its mid-2026 launch.
