About Truxton Extreme

The release date for Truxton Extreme was July 29, 2026, and it is available exclusively for PC. This revival shifts the classic vertical shooter series into a fully 3D environment while keeping the distinct mechanical aesthetic of its 1980s roots. You will pilot a ship armed with four signature weapon types, including the Thunder Laser and Homing Shot, to dismantle swarms of insect-like metal organisms and massive bosses.

The inclusion of a manga-style Story Mode alongside the traditional Arcade Mode suggests a significant push toward modernizing the package for a broader audience. Having the original composer return to remix the soundtrack adds genuine authenticity to this technical upgrade, ensuring the rhythm-heavy shooting remains intact. Shmup veterans and fans of the Tatsujin legacy should play this immediately for its blend of retro mechanics and fresh visuals.
