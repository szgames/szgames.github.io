About Scorched Dominion

The release date for Scorched Dominion is July 19, 2026, for PC. This dark fantasy strategy title moves away from standard elimination goals, instead tasking players with hitting a specific Power target through a combination of held territory and unit-generated assets. You manage a tight action economy, commanding only three units per turn across a tactical board where positioning merchants and farmers is as vital as deploying your front-line warriors.

With five distinct factions and a roster ranging from stealthy rogues to dragons, the depth lies in how you sequence movement and abilities to disrupt enemy growth. The inclusion of local two-player battles and five AI difficulty settings suggests a focused, competitive experience built for those who prefer deliberate, territory-focused planning over raw attrition. Fans of turn-based tactics should keep this on their radar for its technical approach to resource management. It is a solid choice to wishlist for strategy players who value board control over simple combat.
