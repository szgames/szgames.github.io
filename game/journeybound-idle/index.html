About Journeybound Idle

The release date for Journeybound Idle was August 7, 2026, and it launched on PC. This fantasy idle RPG tasks you with a monumental climb from an F-Rank rookie to an S-Rank legend within the Adventurer's Guild.

It emphasizes a truly long-haul progression, letting you chop, mine, fish, and craft your way through vast territories, clearing 114 quests and discovering over 1,300 items. With a desktop widget to keep the game running in the background, this is for players who want a deep, hands-off experience. Check this out if you enjoy persistent progression systems.
