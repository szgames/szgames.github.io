About Alphabetto's

The release date for Alphabetto's on PC is July 23, 2026. This indie strategy title casts you as a gambler in a silent library, playing against literary icons to save their stories from being forgotten by chaining and mutating letters in a high-stakes competition.

The central tension lies in the shared deck mechanic, where every powerful card you add to your collection is just as likely to end up in your opponent's hand. To mitigate this risk, you can sign cards to force rivals to pay royalties whenever they use your best assets against you. It is a clever twist on traditional deckbuilding that requires you to balance the strength of your cards with the danger of arming your enemy.

Buy this if you want a tense, word-based roguelite that punishes reckless deck bloating.
