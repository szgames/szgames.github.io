About Force Escape

The release date for Force Escape on PC is July 22, 2026. This minimalist physics arcade game tasks you with using a force ring to sweep, smash, and clear obstacles for an ascending shuttle across 55 handcrafted levels. While it is built on a simple premise of protecting a fragile craft, the difficulty is tuned for high-precision play and constant restarts.

The developer notes that a perfect run takes under fifteen minutes, but the challenge lies in the unforgiving speed and physics interactions that make survival a feat of coordination. This Steam version has been rebuilt specifically for widescreen monitors and includes a 2x speed mode for those who have mastered the standard velocity. It is a focused, mechanical test of skill with no secondary systems or filler to distract from the core loop.

Buy this if you want a short, punishing arcade experience that demands perfection.
