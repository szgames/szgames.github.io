About Iron Graves

The release date for Iron Graves is July 21, 2026, on PC. Developed by Commoditech, this tactical roguelike places you in the remote command seat of a salvage shuttle, directing a squad of drones to scavenge derelict military and industrial vessels for vital fuel and scrap.

The gameplay revolves around a high-stakes terminal interface where you manage limited resources and monitor sensors to navigate procedurally generated hulls. Because you rely on motion scanners and restricted camera feeds rather than direct sight, the tension comes from interpreting data while something stalks your units in the dark. With permadeath for every drone and the constant threat of running out of fuel, one bad breach or a hardware failure can end a run instantly. Strategy fans should keep an eye on this for its punishing resource management and thick atmospheric horror. Wishlist this if you want a tense, technical scavenging sim.
