About Shadow Slayer

The release date for Shadow Slayer is August 11, 2026 on PC. This action roguelite flips the standard auto-battler formula by making stealth, not firepower, your primary weapon. Every move matters as you navigate darkness, using environmental factors like weather and biomes to hide from enemies with keen senses.

Murder has consequences: corpses must be dragged away, patrols respond to discoveries, and even birds or bloodhounds can betray your presence. You will need to master silent takedowns and environmental manipulation to survive. Beneath the stealth layer, 33 unique hunters, auto-firing weapons with evolutions, passive powers, and character skill trees promise deep replayability. Anyone looking for a fresh take on the roguelite genre, prioritizing unseen kills over pure damage, should mark Shadow Slayer for purchase.
