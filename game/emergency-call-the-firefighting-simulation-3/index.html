About Emergency Call - The Firefighting Simulation 3

The release date for Emergency Call - The Firefighting Simulation 3 is August 5, 2026, launching exclusively on PC. This simulation puts players directly into a German professional fire department, where 24-hour shifts involve not just responding to emergencies but also continuous training and drills. You'll progress from an attack crew member to an engineer, qualifying for various emergency vehicles and roles through dedicated practice.

The real hook lies in the detailed career progression, requiring theoretical knowledge, practical drills with tools like the Halligan, and physical fitness to gain new responsibilities. This focus on realistic operational readiness and diverse roles should appeal to simulation enthusiasts looking for an authentic firefighting career. If you crave a detailed, role-playing firefighting career, this is a definite wishlist addition.
