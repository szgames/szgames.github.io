About Papercraft Tactics

The release date for Papercraft Tactics was July 24, 2026, for PC. This tactical roguelike combines a paper-theater aesthetic with a sophisticated grid-inventory system where equipment layout dictates every action in battle.

Success depends on wiring your backpack. Instead of simple stat boosts, you must physically rotate and align items so their inputs and outputs trigger chain reactions during the turn-based auto-combat. You are essentially building a mechanical engine inside a grid, using a handful of tactical cards to nudge the results when the automated queue needs a manual override. The challenge lies in managing the spatial constraints of the grid while drafting gear that scales effectively through multiple runs.

Buy this if you want a deep, spatial puzzle game hidden inside a run-based battler.
