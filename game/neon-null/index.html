About Neon Null

The release date for Neon Null is July 30, 2026, launching exclusively on PC.

This fast-paced 2D action game drops you into a fractured cosmos, where precise movement and rapid-fire combat are essential to survive procedurally generated encounters and dense enemy waves. As reality collapses into a digital void, you'll pilot your craft, recover data, and unlock new upgrades, ensuring progress continues even after a failed attempt. Expect a demanding experience where every run offers new challenges and unlocks, making it a compelling wishlist for fans of reactive, high-skill shooters.
