About Rubinite

The release date for Rubinite was July 22, 2026, on PC. This dark fantasy action title focuses on a high-stakes boss rush structure where typical hack-and-slash aggression is ineffective against the supernatural Blood Recipients. You play as Ruby, an exiled princess who must systematically dismantle a series of cultists and undead entities to reclaim her fallen kingdom.

Combat centers on the Focus mechanic, a system that forces you to identify specific structural weaknesses before landing lethal thrust attacks. Each victory allows you to absorb the blood of your enemies to craft talismans, which act as the primary progression system for customizing your build between encounters. It is a focused, pixel-art experience designed for players who prefer mastering distinct boss patterns over clearing rooms of minor enemies. Buy this if you want a punishing boss-centric challenge with technical combat.
