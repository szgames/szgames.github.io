About Dungeon Tactics: Idle, Incremental, Autobattler

The release date for Dungeon Tactics: Idle, Incremental, Autobattler was July 27, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC.

This indie title puts a tactical spin on the idle dungeon auto-battler formula, allowing players to assign adventurers to frontline or backline roles within a team of up to six. The core appeal lies in its "Always on top" feature, designed to run unobtrusively in a screen corner while you focus on other tasks. As your team automatically fights and gains experience, you'll unlock new heroes, skills, and explore a deep upgrade tree, with the unique ability to mark upgrade objectives for easy cost tracking. If you enjoy persistent progression that rewards strategic setup more than active play, this PC release is available now.
