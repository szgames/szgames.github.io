About Dungeons & Dummies

The release date for Dungeons & Dummies was July 30, 2026, and it is now available on PC. This adventure throws players into a deep, ever-changing dungeon, playable solo or with up to 7 friends, where the push for more loot and power defines every run.

Greed is actively encouraged, and survival is optional as you make "one more floor" decisions against increasingly tough enemies and scarce supplies. Each descent is a calculated risk, but back in town, spoils can be sold for better gear and new strategies. Buy this if you crave chaotic co-op dungeon crawling with high stakes and serious risk-reward choices.
