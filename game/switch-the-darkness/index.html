About Switch the Darkness

The release date for Switch the Darkness was July 23, 2026, for PC, and it is now available. This psychological horror title strips away inventory management and combat, focusing entirely on memory and observation. You are trapped in an domestic loop where your only task is to identify subtle changes in your environment. If the house remains exactly as you remember it, you go to sleep; if a painting shifts or a sound changes, you must flip the light switch to survive.

The experience is designed for short, high-tension sessions lasting between 15 and 60 minutes. Success depends on your ability to spot unpredictable anomalies in furniture placement or ambient lighting, with a single error resetting the entire cycle. It is a pure test of focus for those who prefer escalating tension over action. Fans of observation-based horror can now pick this up for a quick, concentrated hit of paranoia.
