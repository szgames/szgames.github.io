About AfterClerk

The release date for AfterClerk was July 23, 2026, on PC. Sitting at a desk in the afterlife's bureaucracy, you act as a soul-weigher tasked with reading the ledgers of the dead and sealing their fate for Heaven or Hell. Every judgment you pass during your eighteen-shift tenure directly impacts your character's forgotten past and the ending of your own story.

This narrative RPG moves beyond simple binary choices by introducing a harsh Director who enforces a tightening doctrine, turning acts of mercy into genuine risks for your career and safety. While the game features over ninety individual souls to judge, the tension lies in how your specific verdicts gradually reveal the identity you lost before death. This is a claustrophobic, choice-heavy simulation where the records you keep are as dangerous as the souls you condemn.

Pick this up if you want a dark, high-stakes management RPG that tests your conscience against institutional rules.
