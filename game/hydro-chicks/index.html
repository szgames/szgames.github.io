About Hydro Chicks

The release date for Hydro Chicks is July 20, 2026, and it is coming exclusively to PC. This physics-driven parkour title tasks you with piloting a sea scooter up a treacherous river to the summit of Hydro Mountain, fighting against strong currents and massive waves.

Rather than a standard racer, this is a high-stakes ascent where precision matters. You can select one of five different characters and navigate a hand-crafted mountain path filled with geysers and coin challenges. While geysers offer significant shortcuts, they require clearing specific hurdles that might just send you tumbling back to the bottom. Success relies on learning how the wacky water physics interact with your scooter to avoid the hazards scattered throughout each region. It is a punishing, vertical climb designed for those who enjoy mastery through failure and persistence. Buy this if you want a frustrating but rewarding aquatic challenge that demands perfect movement.
