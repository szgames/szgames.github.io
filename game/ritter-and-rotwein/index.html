About Ritter & Rotwein

The release date for Ritter and Rotwein is July 20, 2026, for PC. This physics-based action title centers on the precarious movement of a 15th-century knight who is significantly more effective with a blade than he is at staying upright. You must navigate levels while managing a ragdoll character whose stability is constantly undermined by the very wine used to heal injuries.

Success depends on balancing heavy late-medieval plate armor and weapon weight against unpredictable momentum. While the single-player campaign focuses on mastering the awkward movement required to climb, jump, and fight through increasingly difficult stages, the inclusion of local and remote play PvP turns the mechanical instability into a competitive hurdle. It is a game of deliberate clumsiness where the fastest, most honorable path is rarely a straight line.

Buy this if you want a high-stakes physics comedy that rewards precise timing within a chaotic movement system.
