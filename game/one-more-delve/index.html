About One More Delve

The release date for One More Delve is July 23, 2026, on PC for VR headsets. This dungeon crawler moves away from floaty arcade combat, instead focusing on a physics-heavy system where the weight of your axes, shields, and two-handed swords dictates the flow of every encounter.

Success relies on technical parrying and blocking rather than simple button mashing, making the three-player co-op runs feel high-stakes. Between expeditions, the social hub provides a space to craft specific gear sets and refine your build before tackling randomized floors and their final bosses. It is a focused extraction-style loop built for players who want tactile feedback and dismemberment mechanics in their dungeon runs.

Serious VR enthusiasts looking for a weighted melee experience should wishlist this for its July launch.
