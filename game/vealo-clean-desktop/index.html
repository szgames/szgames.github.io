About Vealo - Clean Desktop

The release date for Vealo - Clean Desktop was August 5, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This utility application provides a unique way to declutter your workspace, tucking all desktop icons into a customizable panel that opens on demand. It aims to offer a unified aesthetic for your Windows setup, allowing you to personalize themes, wallpapers, cursors, and even keyboard lighting.

What sets Vealo apart is its integrated Studio feature, allowing a complete overhaul of your Windows appearance with ready-made palettes, live effects like rain or snow, and a gallery of 128 wallpapers, all without requiring online accounts. Every detail, from the taskbar to light/dark mode switching, follows your chosen theme for a cohesive look. For PC users seeking a streamlined desktop and deep visual customization, Vealo is a purchase to seriously consider.
