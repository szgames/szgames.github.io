About Vaultix

The release date for Vaultix is July 23, 2026, on PC. This strategy title focuses entirely on the tension of a push-your-luck loop where you crack open vaults to find rare loot or instant failure. Your main objective is to manage limited inventory space and decide exactly when to bank your winnings before the escalating danger causes a total loss of progress.

The most distinctive mechanic is the Corruption system, which offers a desperate second chance to survive a game over by sacrificing a portion of your current character progression. Outside of individual runs, you will trade stolen goods on a black market to purchase permanent talent upgrades that improve survival odds and jackpot frequency. It is a focused experience built for those who enjoy tight inventory management and the constant risk of losing a big haul.

Secure your loot and buy this if you want a quick, high-tension strategy loop.
