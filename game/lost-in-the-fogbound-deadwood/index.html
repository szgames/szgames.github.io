About Lost in the Fogbound Deadwood

The release date for Lost in the Fogbound Deadwood was July 23, 2026, on PC. This first-person survival title drops you into a dense forest with a strict sixty-minute timer and almost no visibility. Success depends on navigating by instinct and scavenging for keys to unlock new paths before the clock runs out.

Resources are intentionally scarce, forcing a constant choice between engaging hostile enemies or conserving limited ammunition for later threats. The lack of an in-game map makes spatial awareness your most important skill, as the fog hides traps and hazards that can end a run instantly. It is a high-pressure extraction loop designed for players who want their orientation and resource management tested under a hard deadline. Pick this up if you enjoy the tension of navigating hostile spaces without hand-holding.
