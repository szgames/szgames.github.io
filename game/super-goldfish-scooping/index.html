About Super Goldfish Scooping

The release date for Super Goldfish Scooping is July 22, 2026, on PC. This arcade-style action title transforms the traditional Japanese festival activity into a fast-paced competition featuring power-ups, environmental hazards, and boss-sized fish. You can choose between solo score attacks or local matches for up to four players across diverse arenas ranging from zen rock gardens to chaotic, toy-filled stages.

The inclusion of items that modify your poi size and movement speed shifts this away from a simple simulation and into the territory of a party brawler. You will have to manage the water's flow while dodging explosive pufferfish and predatory sharks that can ruin a high-scoring chain. It is a niche but focused pick for groups looking for a quick, competitive session. Buy this if you want a frantic, easy-to-learn multiplayer game for your next digital game night.
