About The Great Ebony Clock

The release date for The Great Ebony Clock is July 27, 2026, for PC. This point-and-click adventure traps you in a temporal loop inside your own home, forcing you to manipulate an ancient artifact to rewrite the hours and escape an endless Sunday.

The game centers on a clever localized time-shifting mechanic where the house remains the same, but inserting Ebony Faces into the clock alters the contents of rooms and furniture across different moments of the day. You are joined by a suspicious Time Raven whose true motives are hidden, adding a layer of narrative tension to the puzzle-solving. The loop structure rewards careful investigation of how objects move through the hours rather than traditional reflex-based challenges. It is a focused, single-location mystery built for those who enjoy methodical environmental puzzles and finding every hidden ending.

Wishlist this if you want a cozy but high-stakes temporal puzzle box.
