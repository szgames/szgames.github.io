About Peerless Beauty Detective: Alice

The release date for Peerless Beauty Detective: Alice is July 23, 2026, on PC. Taking the role of the titular detective, you are tasked with investigating a suspicious death at the Ai Qing Apartment, where a woman was found dead in her unit with no signs of a struggle. This third-person investigation focuses on navigating the environment and pieceing together clues from task logs and messages to uncover why the victim's husband was absent during the incident.

The gameplay centers on environmental puzzles and character interactions within a single-location murder mystery. While the setup follows a traditional detective structure, success depends on meticulous checking of hints and messages to trigger specific plot events. It is a straightforward simulation of crime-scene investigation for those who prefer methodical clue-gathering over action. Pick this up if you want a short, focused mystery to solve.
