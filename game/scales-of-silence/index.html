About Scales of Silence

The release date for Scales of Silence is July 22, 2026, launching exclusively for PC. This local multiplayer title reinvents the classic snake formula by making your body length your literal health bar, allowing up to eight players to compete on a single shared screen. You can use a mix of controllers and keyboard zones to get everyone into the match instantly.

The game distinguishes itself with a roster of 30 serpents, each featuring unique stats for speed, acceleration, and growth rates that change how you control space. Beyond the standard PvP royale and score battles, there is a two-player cooperative campaign that explores a war between snake factions and a mysterious encroaching darkness. Success depends on grinding down your opponent's tail while managing your own shrinking hitbox under pressure.

Grab this if you have a group for couch gaming and want a high-speed twist on an arcade classic.
