About Chop Chop Inc.

The release date for Chop Chop Inc. was August 7, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC. This adventure game challenges players to build a sprawling timber and furniture business from the ground up, all after being unceremoniously fired from a corporate job.

It focuses on automating production lines, processing logs into planks, and crafting quirky, profitable items like wooden PCs. Explore a handcrafted world, discover new tree types, and improve your character with strength and stamina upgrades. Anyone who enjoys a satisfying automation loop with a dash of quirky humor can now play Chop Chop Inc.
