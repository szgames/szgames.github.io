About Cat Cafe

The release date for Cat Cafe is July 22, 2026, on PC. While it presents as a relaxing simulation about serving feline customers, the actual experience is a high-speed time management game where you must balance efficient order fulfillment with a looming debt owed to the mafia. You inherited the shop from your grandfather, but keeping the business afloat requires managing fish stocks and customer patience under strict time pressure.

Success depends on your ability to handle a chaotic kitchen while dealing with a luck-based wheel mechanic that can shift the difficulty of your shift at a moment's notice. It is a frantic take on the restaurant management genre that swaps cozy vibes for mechanical tension and high-stakes strategy. Those looking for a stressful management loop with a quirky narrative edge should wishlist this one for its mid-2026 launch.
