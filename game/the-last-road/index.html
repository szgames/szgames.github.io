About The Last Road

The release date for The Last Road was August 24, 2026, and it is now available exclusively on PC. This is a narrative-driven casual strategy RPG where you play as The Driver, bound by oath to deliver vital supplies across a war-torn fantasy realm.

Success hinges on meticulous resource management of supplies, rations, and scrap across five unique zones, each with its own story and challenges. Semi-randomized events and choice-based outcomes demand careful planning, forcing you to balance risky exploration for resources against resting to survive. For players seeking a focused strategic journey with high replayability and branching narratives, The Last Road is a strong purchase.
