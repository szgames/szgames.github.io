About Infinite Ruins

The release date for Infinite Ruins is July 27, 2026, arriving for PC. This strategy title shifts the focus from manual clicking to high-level automation, as you deploy specialized golems to carve through elemental layers of ice, fire, and lightning. It is an incremental game built around the transition from hands-on placement to a fully self-sustaining resource machine.

The central hook is managing the volatility of the ruins, where lightning strikes can clear entire sections and resource vortices threaten to pull your haul away. Success depends on how well you sequence golem abilities to trigger chain reactions that clear the screen while you are away. It strips away typical survival pressure to focus entirely on scaling your output and optimizing your efficiency across infinite floors.

Grab this if you want a low-stress automation loop focused on rapid progression and resource management.
