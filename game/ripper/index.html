About Ripper

The release date for Ripper is July 24, 2026, for PC. This top-down shooter moves away from static cover by making every building, wall, and piece of furniture fully destructible, forcing you to reshape the map to gain an advantage.

Visibility is tied to a strict line-of-sight fog of war system where blowing a hole through a wall doesn't just create a new path—it instantly reveals any enemies lurking behind it. Between the wave-based survival mode, objective-based attacks, and traditional PvP deathmatch, the focus remains on high-speed tactical demolition and reacting to a shifting environment. With a built-in level editor for custom arenas, the longevity of the game depends on how creatively the community can weaponize the physics engine.

Fans of high-stakes tactical shooters should wishlist this for the environmental destruction alone.
