About Stuff to do

The release date for Stuff to do is July 23, 2026, on PC. This surreal horror experience puts you inside a piece of abandonware to track down three developers who vanished while building it.

Built on a point-and-click foundation reminiscent of Myst, the game uses a minimalist control scheme limited to the mouse and scroll wheel. The challenge is split between logical puzzles and psychological survival, where your perception of the digital space shifts as you interact with the environment. It leans heavily on its retro aesthetic and atmospheric tension rather than traditional menus or UI to drive the investigation. Expect a cryptic path forward that prioritizes observation and environmental storytelling over direct guidance.

A purchase for those seeking a quiet, unsettling dive into an experimental digital rabbit hole.
