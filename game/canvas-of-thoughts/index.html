About Canvas of Thoughts

The release date for Canvas of Thoughts is July 23, 2026, on PC. This 2D point-and-click adventure focuses on the internal life of Evi, an 11-year-old girl navigating daily life through a neurodivergent lens. Instead of typical inventory puzzles, you interact with environments like classrooms and malls by managing sensory input and emotional regulation through patterns and logic.

The game translates mental states into visual and auditory shifts, using a watercolor art style to show how common social situations can become overwhelming or calming. It avoids traditional fail states, opting instead for reflective exploration and object combination that reflects Evi's personal perspective. It is a thoughtful pick for anyone looking for a narrative experience that prioritizes empathy and sensory awareness over fast-paced action. Wishlist this if you want a quiet, perspective-driven story.
