About Shift At Midnight

The release date for Shift At Midnight was July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by Bun Muen and published by Kwalee, this indie simulation combines mundane retail management with high-stakes doppelganger detection.

You and up to two friends run a gas station where the shift loop involves stocking shelves and cleaning floors while cross-referencing customer IDs to spot monsters in disguise. The tension comes from the binary consequences of your detective work: identify the mimic and execute them on the spot, or miss a detail and face a siege. If a non-human entity escapes your screening, the game pivots into a survival horror encounter where you must barricade doors and set traps to survive the night. The inclusion of randomly generated shifts ensures that you cannot simply memorize customer patterns, making communication and observation the primary tools for survival.

Grab this if you want a cooperative horror experience that tests your attention to detail under pressure.
