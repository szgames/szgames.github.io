About Chinese Street Food Legend

The release date for Chinese Street Food Legend was August 14, 2026, and it launched exclusively on PC, where it is available now.

This simulation game puts you in charge of a night market stall, specializing in kaolengmian (grilled cold noodles). While the core loop involves fast, precise cooking—cracking eggs, brushing sauce, flipping noodles—it uniquely intertwines with the poignant stories of your diverse customers. They share joys and struggles, transforming your humble stall into a hub of human connection. The challenge lies in mastering quick reflexes for cooking while absorbing the rich, everyday narratives unfolding around you. Aspiring street food entrepreneurs and fans of narrative-driven simulations can now definitely play this charming experience.
