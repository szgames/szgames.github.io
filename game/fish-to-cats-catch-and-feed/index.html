About Fish to Cats: Catch & Feed

The release date for Fish to Cats: Catch & Feed was July 22, 2026, on PC. This casual indie title combines light resource management with maritime exploration across nine different ocean zones.

You will track down 65 unique fish species to satisfy the residents of Cat Island, using successful feedings to upgrade your fishing capabilities. The inclusion of an idle sidekick system and gacha mechanics suggests a progression loop built around incremental wealth generation and automated collection. The real challenge lies in the rare leviathan encounters, which serve as the primary gatekeepers for high-tier ultimate skills. It is a straightforward loop for those who enjoy filling out a collection log and managing a growing roster of upgrades.

A solid pick for fans of low-stress fishing games who want a clear upgrade path and a whimsical theme.
