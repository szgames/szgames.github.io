About Voxel Shooter

The release date for Voxel Shooter was July 20, 2026 on PC. This side-scrolling roguelite shmup supports up to four players in local co-op or through Steam Remote Play Together, emphasizing a high-stakes economy where a single hit halves your currency multiplier and you only have two health points to spare.

Success depends on managing four elemental weapon types to exploit color-coded enemy weaknesses. You must choose between building a wide arsenal for screen coverage or focusing credits on specific weapons to maximize their power. With 90 upgrades and seven distinct bosses, the challenge scales through ten difficulty tiers that you select as the run progresses. High-intensity arcade fans should check this out for the tight risk-reward loop and local multiplayer chaos.

A strong choice for groups looking for a demanding couch co-op challenge.
