About Dirty Business

The release date for Dirty Business was July 22, 2026, on PC. Developed by Plan Dirty Games, this simulation title tasks you with managing an illicit manufacturing hub from a hidden underground hangar while coordinating city-wide distribution through a secret cartel airstrip.

The game distinguishes itself by focusing on the logistics of a multi-product criminal enterprise, requiring you to balance the production of narcotics, counterfeit currency, and weaponry simultaneously. Success depends on how you scale from small elevator shipments to massive aerial deliveries while managing a hired crew of street dealers and chemists. Whether playing solo or in co-op, the challenge lies in optimizing your front companies to move high-volume orders without alerting the authorities. This is a management sim for those who prefer logistics with a dark, high-stakes edge.

Aspiring kingpins can now pick this up.
