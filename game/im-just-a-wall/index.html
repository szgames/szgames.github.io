About I'm Just a Wall

The release date for I'm Just a Wall on PC was July 27, 2026, and the game is now available. This indie title scales the familiar prop hunt formula to accommodate up to 30 players in a high-stakes game of hide and seek where every contestant is a cat in disguise.

Success depends on manual camouflage rather than simple shape-shifting, as you must use a brush to paint over your feline features to match the randomly generated terrain of the island. The inclusion of treasure chests forces hiders to move and expose themselves for points, while a proximity-based voice chat adds a layer of tension for those trying to stay silent as seekers close in. Because caught cats join the hunting team, rounds escalate quickly as the odds shift against the survivors.

Pick this up if you want a social, high-player-count party game with a creative twist on environmental concealment.
