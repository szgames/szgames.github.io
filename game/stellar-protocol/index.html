About Stellar Protocol

The release date for Stellar Protocol was July 23, 2026, on PC. This strategy title strips away the hand-holding common in modern puzzles, forcing you to deduce the behavior of unknown blocks and environmental hazards through pure experimentation across 99 hand-crafted levels.

Survival depends on a rigid three-strike system and strict move limits that punish reckless play. Because the game refuses to explain its own mechanics, you must treat every failure as a data point to optimize your path and secure a high score. Success requires a methodical approach where you observe a new threat, test its logic, and execute the perfect sequence to reach the exit. Strategy enthusiasts who enjoy solving complex systems without a manual should play this immediately.
